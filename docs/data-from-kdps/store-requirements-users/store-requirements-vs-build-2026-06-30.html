3 · What this changes — three calls KDPS needs to make

Most of this list is either already built or already designed and queued. But three items in this requirement reverse or extend decisions we'd already taken. They're worth settling in writing before we build, because they change the size of the project.

1. Does KDPS own the customer, or does the POS? Today's design says the POS (Ten Software) owns the customer and the bill, and KDPS holds no customer master. "Customer Search by name/phone", "reprint the bill", and a customer-facing "member" list all assume the opposite — that KDPS now holds customers and sales. This is a real expansion (it was previously left to the POS). It's do-able, but it needs a clear yes.
2. Is "Attendance" in scope now? HR / Attendance & Payroll was parked for later by decision. Bringing it forward means a new module plus biometric hardware and its integration — a separate piece of work from the store/stock system. Fine to do; just needs to be added to scope deliberately, not assumed.
3. What exactly is "Member Details"? Two readings, two different builds:
  1. Loyalty / club customers — repeat shoppers, spend targets, offers. (Currently deferred.)
  2. Staff / salespeople — contact + bank details, monthly sales target vs achievement, growth/de-growth, performance pie. (The bank-details + target/achievement wording points here.)
These are not the same module. Please confirm which one — or both — before we design it.

Answered 25 July 2026 — it is reading 2, staff. The sketch settles it on its own terms: Customer Search is item 6 and Member Details is item 7 of the same list, each defined separately, so the sketch already treats customer and member as different things. Bank details and a monthly sales target belong to a salesperson, not a shopper, and the Reports list asks for a "member-wise report" beside an "item-wise report" — salesperson-wise sales. Loyalty stays deferred and "the POS owns the customer" stands unchanged.

What it costs: this makes the item bigger, not smaller. Member Details is an employee master holding bank details — it belongs to the Attendance & Payroll module parked in question 2 above, and carries that module's staff-PII duty with it. Access is settled: a store manager manages their own store's members; a cashier keeps their own attendance only (issue #96).
