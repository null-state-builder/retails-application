How these numbers were built

Method & honest caveats

  • Stock @ MRP = stock qty × MRP from the stock file. Cost value = qty × the file's Rate column.
  • Actual sales = the Net Amount column, i.e. after discount and net of 946 returned pieces (₹18.5 L).
  • Sale-through = sold qty ÷ (sold qty + stock qty). Opening stock was not supplied, so this uses the closing snapshot.
  • Cost of goods sold is estimated. The sales export carries no cost column, so each sold line was matched to the stock file for its cost-to-MRP ratio: barcode first, then brand+item, then item, then brand.
  • Match quality: 29% of cost matched on exact barcode, 64% on brand+item, 7% on item, 0.3% on brand/overall average.
  • Carry bags excluded — 6,447 lines / 7,027 pcs of packaging, ₹1,941 of sales. They are not merchandise.
  • The stock file's own grand-total row was dropped (it otherwise doubles every total).
  • Item names were normalised across the two files (Sarees→Saree, Trousers→Trouser, Sweat Shirt→Sweatshirt) so stock and sales line up.
  • Categories come from the stock file. Around 0.2% of sold lines carried a junk category code and were classified from their item name instead.
  • Not included: rent, salary, power and other running costs. Margin here is gross — goods only.
  • Gift / promotional stock (145 pcs, ₹9.4 L at MRP) carries an inflated tag against a near-zero cost — it is kept out of the brand buy-margin column so it does not flatter the brands.
  • Free/gift items (224 pcs, ₹5.1 L at MRP) show as 100% discount and a full-cost loss — that is the real cost of the gift scheme.
One data-quality flag. The sales export's own trailing total row does not reconcile with its line items, and the stock file's total row is ₹4,84,973 above the sum of its rows. Everything in this report is computed from the line items, which are internally consistent.
