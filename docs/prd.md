# RetailsOps — Unified Retail ERP Platform

**Standalone Overall Product Requirements Document**\
**Version:** 2.2 standalone edition · **Status:** Version 2.0 approved by Anand Kumar on 24 September 2026; version 2.1 records decisions of 25 September and version 2.2 records the SO-01 store reconciliation of 29 September 2026. Open Section 15 questions block only the capabilities they name. Product scope and the Django/Python and React/TypeScript technology direction (Section 16) were approved in earlier editions

RetailsOps is a unified retail ERP for multi-site retail chains. It provides one connected operating platform for:

- **Merchandise and inventory** — booking through physical receipt, AI-assisted parsing of booking documents, invoices, external PT files and delivery evidence with provenance and human review, stock evidence, transfers, and sellable store stock
- **Store and warehouse operations** — point of sale (including offline billing), sales, discounts and promotions, returns, adjustments, and cash reconciliation
- **Finance and commercial control** — procurement, accounting, payables and receivables, tax evidence, profitability, Tally XML and reconciliation
- **People and partners** — HR, attendance, targets, incentives, payroll, payslips, staff movement, franchise, and partner reporting
- **Analysis and intelligence** — governed metrics and operational reporting, AI-assisted analysis of store and warehouse stock, demand forecasts and replenishment planning, and human-reviewed recommendations

Shared foundations—tenant isolation, product identity, immutable evidence, access control, and audit—apply across every capability.

## 2. Business context and problems



### 2.1 Operating context

RetailsOps serves retail chains that book merchandise, receive it at warehouses or stores, make stock official through PT, move it, sell it at store POS, and analyse network performance. A tenant may operate multiple legal entities, sites, brands, tax registrations and franchise relationships. A tenant may activate only a subset of capabilities.

Tenant-specific facts—site counts, volumes, current tools, commercial terms and baselines—are discovered per tenant. They are not universal product rules.

### 2.2 Problems the product must address


| Problem                                                | Required improvement                                                                                   |
| ------------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| External codes are inconsistent or reused              | Use a stable internal SKU; aliases map to it; never silently merge different goods                     |
| Supplier documents are unstructured or incomplete      | AI-assisted parsing into a draft PT; human review; originals and provenance retained                   |
| Arrival, invoice, booking and workplace are confused   | Record the actual arrival site; keep booking, invoice/count and GRN/PT comparisons separate            |
| Quantity and value are posted more than once           | Count once at GRN; establish value once when the receipt PT becomes official                           |
| Historic receipt files become editable stock balances  | Official PT is immutable; current stock is derived from later movements                                |
| Goods appear sellable before acceptance                | Sell only official-PT-covered, accepted, unheld stock at the eligible site                             |
| Movements lose cost and receipt origin                 | Keep receipt lineage through transfer, transit, return and correction                                  |
| Internet failure disrupts billing or duplicates bills  | Durable offline billing with explicit limits and safe reconciliation                                   |
| Reports hide stale or missing data                     | Show freshness, definition, coverage and reproducible results                                          |
| Automation posts stock or money without a person       | AI proposes; humans approve; a complete manual path remains                                            |
| Booking, invoice and PT entry is repetitive            | Use AI-assisted extraction to create reviewable drafts from source documents                           |
| Receipt differences are found too late                 | Reconcile receipt claims, count, coverage and authorised dispositions during every receipt PT approval |
| Brand/vendor return opportunities are missed           | Track communicated return windows, eligible stock, deadlines and return progress                       |
| Store and brand profitability is unclear               | Show governed store, brand and brand-by-store profit and margin views                                  |
| Attendance, incentives and payroll are manually joined | Calculate approved attendance, achievement, incentive, payroll and payslip results                     |
| Site opening and closure is fragmented                 | Use controlled readiness, stock disposition, staff movement and final reconciliation                   |




### 2.3 Tenant discovery boundary

Each tenant confirms its journey, roles, sites, commercial arrangements, evidence, regulatory applicability and success baselines. Discovery from one retailer is illustrative only. A sample accounting package, bank format, date, tax ladder or provider is not a platform-wide requirement.

### 3.2 Mandatory platform success controls


| Control           | Required outcome                                                                                                                            |
| ----------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Traceability      | Every supported stock or money event links to its source document, actor, time, scope and applicable origin.                                |

| Replay safety     | Repeating the same approved command creates no duplicate document, movement, bill, voucher or payment effect.                               |
| Sale eligibility  | Only official-PT-covered, accepted, unheld stock at the correct site is available to sell or allocate.                                      |
| Access isolation  | No user or service can access an unauthorised tenant, entity, site, brand, action or protected field.                                       |
| Reporting trust   | Controlled metrics show their definition version, as-of point, completeness and unavailable inputs.                                         |

## 6. End-to-end business processes

These are intended connected processes. Tenant-specific current-state workflows are supplied during onboarding where required. Each process inherits the shared access, evidence, retry, exception and configuration rules; decision-gated processes remain inactive until their Section 15 decisions close and their acceptance evidence passes.

### 6.1 BP-01 — Configure an organisation and make operations ready

**Trigger / participants:** New tenant, new site or material configuration change; platform administrator, company owner, finance and MDM stewards.

**Flow:** Record the approved business profile and expected scale → establish tenant → legal entities/books/registrations → sites, SBUs and stock locations → merchandise categories and SKU profile → brands/vendors and commercial models → workforce → POS/accounting interfaces → roles and authority → devices/number series → operating policies and dated rules → readiness validation.

**Outcome:** An authorised user can operate only the activated scope with visible configuration provenance. Missing registrations, scopes, formulas, hardware support or approval owners remain setup exceptions; no implicit fallback to another tenant, site or rule.

### 6.2 BP-02 — Record booking intent and track deliveries

**Trigger / participants:** Buyer records intent against one vendor, brand and season.

**Flow:** Upload or capture a booking source document → AI-assisted extraction creates a structured draft with provenance/confidence → buyer reviews and corrects it → authorised confirmation saves the booking → receive one or more independent deliveries → link valid receipts/reversals → display outstanding → explicitly short-close when authorised. Manual booking entry remains available.

**Outcome / exceptions:** Partial delivery is not automatically shipment shortage. Over-receipt requires a remark; booking-less receipt is permitted. A later booking link is dated and cannot rewrite receipt evidence. Example: S/M/L booked at ten each, delivered at five, then three, then two each, leaves outstanding totals 15, 6 and 0.

### 6.3 BP-03 — Receive goods and establish counted stock

**Trigger / participants:** Goods physically arrive with available invoice/vendor evidence; receiving counter and data-entry operator.

**Flow:** Record actual arrival site/time/actors → count by style and size with scan or typed input → compare this invoice's claimed quantity with this count → record good, damaged and wrong/rejected goods → issue quantity-only GRN into receiving hold/quarantine → notify warehouse operator.

**Outcome / exceptions:** Physical truth is recorded even when commercial documentation is incomplete. No cost layer, tax posting, payable or return allowance is created by count. Booking outstanding, invoice/count discrepancy and GRN/PT reconciliation stay separate. Corrections require counter-GRN evidence.

### 6.4 BP-04 — Prepare, reconcile and approve the official PT

**Trigger / participants:** GRN requires PT; authorised preparer, MDM reviewer and PT approver.

**Flow:** Canonical upload, typed/pasted entry or AI-assisted parsing of an external PT, invoice or other supported source produces a canonical draft → preparer reviews/corrects it → submit the reviewed draft revision → compare invoice, GRN, existing coverage and proposed PT immediately → resolve discrepancies through authorised dispositions → approver approves eligible submitted quantities → approval atomically makes the PT official, issues its number/version and opens receipt value/cost layers.

**Outcome / exceptions:** A GRN has at most one primary receipt PT plus supplemental PTs for previously uncovered quantities accepted later; each PT identity has at most one live official version. Reconciliation preserves truthful invoice/count differences and requires authorised dispositions for all quantities. Clean quantities may proceed while other quantities remain explicitly held; invalid submitted rows cannot be silently skipped. Approval and officialisation are one atomic event for the submitted PT. They add value, not a second GRN quantity, and do not release receiving hold. Failed intake does not partially post. Unsafe reversal is refused; reissue requires fresh review and approval. See R-REC-005 and R-INV-012.

### 6.5 BP-05 — Make directly received store stock sellable

**Trigger / participants:** Store goods have an official covering receipt PT; store receiver/manager.

**Flow:** Scan actual identity and origin → record accepted good quantity and discrepancies → place in floor/backstore → release only eligible accepted quantity → update labelled ATS.

**Outcome / exceptions:** Goods physically staying at the store need no invented warehouse hop. Registered barcode alone does not establish eligibility. Held origins remain unavailable even if another origin of the same SKU is accepted. Incomplete scanning is not a short-close.

### 6.6 BP-06 — Allocate, dispatch and receive internal transfers

**Trigger / participants:** Available accepted source stock is required elsewhere; allocator, source operator and destination receiver.

**Flow:** Select eligible source quantities/origins → create derived transfer PT → approve/reserve → dispatch to transit → record physical arrival → receive against that same PT → accept actual good quantity → put away → release eligible destination ATS.

**Outcome / exceptions:** Reservation reduces source availability; dispatch and receipt are separate atomic events. Destination visibility is pending receipt, not destination stock. Partial, damaged, wrong, short and excess deliveries follow owned exception routes. Original quantity/cost/ticket/receipt links are conserved. E-way documentation status is independent of physical progress. One approved transfer may be fulfilled through several dispatches; each dispatch is received as one whole shipment with shortage and excess recorded (R-INV-006). If dispatched goods come back after a failed delivery, record actual receipt at the source, linked to the original movement and dispatch, preserving departure, return arrival, quantity, condition, actor and evidence; do not delete the dispatch or falsely record destination receipt. Returned goods follow the receiving/condition rules at the site that actually receives them, and returned quarantined goods remain quarantined. Returning goods does not count as successful delivery to the intended destination or vendor. Transport-provider handling of the failed delivery itself and any transport-rerouting workflow are out of scope.

### 6.7 BP-07 — Price, bill, collect and reconcile an offline sale

**Trigger / participants:** Authorised cashier scans merchandise at an enabled till.

**Flow:** Open authorised session → resolve scoped alias and eligible site/origin → calculate price/offer/tax with applicable approved versions → record salesperson and permitted optional customer details → collect allowed tenders → durably finalise immutable bill and number → optionally place sold goods into billed-retained custody for later pickup/delivery → print/retry receipt → upload safely → reconcile stock, rules, tenders and external outcomes.

**Outcome / exceptions:** Offline bills survive supported interruption/restart tests; transport retries cannot create a second bill. Billed-retained goods leave ATS immediately and remain linked to the customer, bill, site, custody location and final handover/cancellation/return outcome. Shared-balance tenders require live authority. Unknown/ambiguous/ineligible stock is refused. Original bill contents are never repriced on sync. Independent offline oversell versus FIFO/cost allocation remains blocked by OQ-30, not solved by invented receipt layers.

### 6.8 BP-08 — Return, exchange, adjust or reverse safely

**Trigger / participants:** Customer return, stock discrepancy, damaged item or correction request; cashier, manager, inventory controller and relevant approver.

**Flow:** Locate original bill/document/evidence → verify policy, eligibility and prior consumption → approve required action → issue linked counter/return/adjustment → resolve inventory condition and permitted value/tender effects → retain immutable history and any refused action.

**Outcome / exceptions:** Bill-backed credit follows amount paid; an exchange prices the new item under applicable live offers. Offline returns require cached original bill and remain subject to cross-till duplicate-return policy. No-bill online returns require configured policy and approval; they must not invent cost origin. Saleable return status requires its own accepted condition.

**Store customer-return policy:** The legal entity has a default customer-return policy; a store may have an explicit override where its operating policy differs, and a store without an override uses its entity default. The policy can allow any supported combination of refund, exchange, store credit and not accepted, so a store may allow both refund and exchange, exchange only, or no acceptance of a specified kind of return. Ordinary customer return (size, fit, colour, changed mind, gift and similar) and defective-item return (the customer says the goods are damaged or defective) are separately configurable cases: for each, configuration decides whether refund, exchange, store credit or not accepted is available and the applicable time limit, so a defective item may have different remedies or time limits from an ordinary return. For each return request the store determines: (1) which entity and store policy applies; (2) whether the request is an ordinary or defective-item case; (3) whether it is within the configured time limit; (4) which customer outcomes the policy permits; (5) the eligible value already paid and not previously returned; and (6) the physical condition and stock destination if the goods are accepted. When a store accepts goods back from a customer, it records the condition of the returned piece. The outcome offered to the customer does not decide whether the returned piece is sellable: an accepted good piece may return to eligible store stock after its condition is accepted, and an accepted damaged or defective piece enters quarantine immediately, whether the customer receives a refund, exchange or store credit. Refusing a customer request means the store has not accepted the goods back into its custody through this flow.

### 6.9 BP-09 — Plan, approve and evaluate promotions

**Trigger / participants:** Brand circular, merchandising plan or AI suggestion; buyer, approver, store users and analytics.

**Flow:** Create draft rule with scope/funding/version → validate conflicts and approved formulas → simulate when supported → human approve → distribute effective rules → freeze applied offer/funding/price state on sale → analyse revenue, discount depth, margin, stock depletion and funding/claims. EOSS uses the same governed flow with its approved season, dates and eligibility.

**Outcome / exceptions:** Suggestions cannot go live automatically. No-discount and authorised stacking rules apply. New stores are not silently enrolled into an existing brand offer. Missing funding, margin-floor inputs or commercial approval prevents activation of the affected rule, not ordinary manual workflows.

### 6.10 BP-10 — Reconcile store cash and bank activity

**Trigger / participants:** Till shift closes, cash movement occurs or statement arrives; store manager, cash custodian and finance.

**Flow:** Capture float, tenders and cash movements → close each shift with a blind cash declaration against expected cash (float plus cash bills), with a reason for any variance → lock the trading date, listing numbering holes, uncosted sales and exceptions → retain shift reports → ingest immutable bank lines with balance proof → code/split narration → match against supported operational evidence → investigate fees, unmatched items and variance → close only under approved controls.

**Outcome / exceptions:** A line is matched at most once; unmatching is a reasoned counter-action. Unknown bank formats are parked. Missing source evidence and unexplained cash remainder remain owned open exceptions, not forced reconciliations.

### 6.11 BP-11 — Approve obligations, pay and reconcile statutory books

**Trigger / participants:** Approved payable/receivable/commercial evidence in an enabled finance scope; finance maker, checker and statutory-book operator.

**Flow:** Establish obligation under approved recognition rules → propose payment run or collection → independent approval → release configured instruction → confirm external outcome → match statement → produce canonical accounting export batch or activated Tally XML output → retain destination acknowledgement → period controls and tie-out.

**Outcome / exceptions:** File generation is not bank payment; export is not accounting-package acceptance. Unknown external outcomes require status reconciliation before another consequential attempt. Receipt evidence is available to finance but does not itself approve a guessed payable or purchase journal. Opening-stock PT creates no books entry.

### 6.12 BP-12 — Record attendance, calculate payroll and issue payslips

**Trigger / participants:** Staff attendance/leave events and period end; employee, store manager and HR.

**Flow:** Record assigned staff and shift → capture supported check-in/out or approved correction → distinguish leave/absence → apply configured attendance policy → calculate target achievement and incentives → calculate payroll under approved rules → reviewer approves → issue payslip → export approved accounting/payment inputs.

**Outcome / exceptions:** Offline attendance retains event and recording time. Location verification is evidence under the configured policy, not proof that fraud is impossible. Staff transfers do not relocate historic attendance. Every payslip retains the approved payroll period, source attendance, achievement, incentive and compensation-rule versions. Statutory PF, ESI, payroll TDS, filing and compliance treatment require OQ-46 to close in Section 15.

### 6.13 BP-13 — Review partner, management and intelligence outcomes

**Trigger / participants:** Daily review, period review or analyst question; owners, managers, franchise/finance and approved analytical users.

**Flow:** Publish reconciled reporting data → compute approved metrics → show activated profitability, return exposure, dead-stock, demand, planning and anomaly surfaces → generate the scheduled daily digest from those computed facts → record scoped AI/ML recommendations → human review → perform any approved business action through the normal operational workflow → retain action/outcome evidence.

**Outcome / exceptions:** Stale/low-quality data, forecasts and estimates are labelled. The intelligence layer cannot post stock or money. Franchise access stays partner/site-scoped. Metric gaps cannot be filled by an LLM inventing numbers.

### 6.14 BP-14 — Transition, recover or exit without losing meaning

**Trigger / participants:** Tenant cutover, service recovery, hosting move or product exit; business owner, inventory/finance and authorised support.

**Flow:** Approve source manifest and cutover boundaries → validate identities/values/conditions → establish opening operational stock → reconcile accepted controls → activate supported operations. For recovery/exit, preserve consistent data, evidence, versions, command identities and outstanding integrations → restore/import → reconcile → authorise resumed writes.

**Outcome / exceptions:** No duplicate openings, invented purchase, rewritten bill or lost pending command. Self-hosting the same platform and replacing the backend are different migration scenarios; no effortless migration claim is made.

### 6.15 BP-15 — Manage a communicated brand/vendor return window

**Trigger / participants:** A brand or vendor communicates a return opportunity or deadline; commercial owner, buyer, inventory controller and return approver.

**Flow:** Retain the original communication → AI-assisted parsing creates a dated draft of eligibility, period and destination → designated person reviews and approves it → system identifies eligible unsold stock → issue configured alerts → create a reviewed return list → execute the normal RTV movement → track receipt confirmation, credit note and unresolved differences.

**Outcome / exceptions:** A return window is not manually frozen on every receipt or PT line. Eligibility is derived from the approved notice and current traceable stock. A changed notice creates a new effective version; it does not rewrite the original communication or completed returns. No stock moves automatically.

### 6.16 BP-16 — Open or close a site safely

**Trigger / participants:** Approved site opening or closure; retail operations, inventory, HR, finance and site transition owner.

**Flow:** For opening, create the site/SBU structure → complete readiness checks → review proposed seed stock → approve transfers → assign staff and devices → confirm stock acceptance and operating readiness. For closure, set closing status/date → stop prohibited new receipts/allocations → classify and approve stock disposition → complete transfers/RTV/write-off → reassign or exit staff → reconcile stock, cash and accounting exceptions → approve closure.

**Outcome / exceptions:** Predictions may propose opening stock or stock disposition but cannot execute movements. A closing site cannot disappear or show false zero stock; unresolved balances and exceptions remain visible until resolved or explicitly approved.

### 6.17 BP-17 — Operate partner reporting and settlement

**Trigger / participants:** A franchise relationship becomes active, an EBO reporting period opens, partner evidence is due or a settlement period closes; partner operator/reporter, partner operations and finance.

**Flow:** Approve the partner/site scope and effective commercial terms → receive site-scoped invoice, DSR, SOH and payment-advice evidence → retain and validate each source → route supported evidence into reconciliation and approved finance workflows → calculate the contractual period result and minimum-guarantee comparison under the frozen terms → review discrepancies → issue the approved statement → retain settlement, acknowledgement and unresolved follow-up evidence.

**Outcome / exceptions:** One partner cannot access another partner's data. Missing uploads, terms or verification remain owned exceptions and prevent unsupported settlement claims. Uploaded totals are evidence, not verified POS transactions. No royalty, revenue-share, minimum-guarantee, payable or receivable result is invented while its commercial basis remains unresolved under OQ-52.

**Delivery boundary: Decision-gated.** Every process is in complete-product scope. Its open policy details must be recorded in Section 15 before implementation; inherited integrity and evidence controls are always mandatory.

### 7.2 Organisation and network

**Default metadata:** Owner Organisation steward; priority Required; status Included. Processes BP-01, BP-14 and BP-16.

**R-ORG-001 — Tenant and legal structure.** Support independent tenants and each tenant's legal entities, tax registrations, books, business units, regions, clusters and sites. Tax registration is not interchangeable with legal entity or site. Cross-tenant access is prohibited; cross-entity operations require approved book/registration treatment, not a site-name assumption. **See OQ-05.**

**R-ORG-002 — Site and SBU masters.** Each site carries canonical accounting code, name, short aliases, city/state/country as applicable, registration linkage, type, counter count, franchise partner if any, status and opening date. Every own multi-brand store names its linked warehouse, which is its default hub for inbound split, store replenishment, quarantine consolidation and vendor return from the network. SBU can denote a site or a brand counter inside a site without changing its fundamental business meaning. Several SBUs per site remain supported and configurable; the first tenant's default is one SBU per site, and a flow for moving one brand out of a store is not in the initial configuration. The product's site-type catalogue is: Office (HQ, Regional Office); Warehouse (DC — central/national distribution centre; RDC — regional forward warehouse); Store (COCO, FOFO, FOCO, SIS — a brand shop-in-shop inside a store, Kiosk — temporary or pop-up, EBO — exclusive brand outlet, often on the brand's own software); Outlet backstore (a store's own stockroom, not a DC); and Virtual sites (in-transit, RTV, damage, sample, e-commerce reserve — notional, not a building). Which of these exist for a tenant, and which brands and policies they carry, is master data. The catalogue itself is fixed product structure, the same for every tenant (Section 15.2). **See OQ-14.**

**R-ORG-003 — Location and stock dimensions.** Support storage location, zone, rack/bin/fixture and independent condition/hold/availability dimensions. Every stock view names whether it shows on-hand, available, reserved, blocked, receiving hold, excess hold, transit, quality hold, quarantine or damage and whether its value basis is quantity, cost or ticket value. Every site has six protected system locations, created automatically: receiving, quarantine, excess hold, RTV hold, custody and transit out. Their display names may be changed; their kind cannot be changed, and they cannot be moved, retired or deleted. The company owner may change any site's location tree; a store manager may change only their own store's tree, and a warehouse operator only warehouse bins. Distribution-centre, concession and virtual site location trees are editable by the company owner only.

**R-ORG-004 — Configuration and lifecycle.** Tenant configuration selects active countries, currencies, site types and workflows. Closing a site or retiring a master preserves its historic references and handles outstanding stock, staff and exceptions through explicit decisions rather than deletion. Retiring a site or SBU requires fresh password confirmation (step-up) after its scoped residual checks pass: outstanding stock, transit, reservations, unvalued goods, open documents or exceptions each block retirement until resolved through their own authorised transition, and there is no residual override. A permission grant scoped to an already-retired site or SBU is refused, and no new brand-bound work may start against a site/brand combination whose SBU is retired. Where a site has several SBUs, open exceptions and documents that carry no brand, and goods with no known brand, block retiring every SBU at that site, while brand-bound records block only their own brand's SBU. A transfer checks the brand of every line at both its source and destination sites, and any line whose site-and-brand SBU is retired refuses the transfer.

**R-ORG-005 — Tenant business profile.** Record the tenant's merchandise categories, SKU-defining profile, expected SKU/brand/site/SBU/staff/document volumes, commercial models, workforce scope, POS operation and accounting interfaces. These are tenant inputs used for configuration and capacity acceptance, not universal product constants. The facts required before booking and receiving are activated are the merchandise categories, the SKU-defining profile and the commercial models; the other facts (expected volumes, workforce scope, POS operation and accounting interfaces) are captured before the capability that reads them is activated. **See OQ-22/OQ-23.**

**R-ORG-006 — Site opening and closure.** Opening requires approved site/SBU structure, roles, devices, stock plan, accepted opening stock and readiness checks. Closing records status/date, blocks configured new receipts or allocation, controls stock disposition and staff movement, and retains unresolved stock, cash, accounting and compliance exceptions until resolved or explicitly approved. **See OQ-51.**

**R-ORG-007 — Default valid-operation presets by site type.** Each site type has an editable default of the operations it may perform: warehouse/DC — receive, hold and transfer; store/concession — the same plus a selectable selling capability; virtual — none. Applying a preset never itself grants operational readiness or activates selling; readiness and selling activation remain separate, explicitly approved steps.

### 7.3 Product identity and canonical profiles

**Default metadata:** Owner MDM steward; priority Critical; status Included. Processes BP-01 and BP-03–BP-09.

**R-MDM-001 — Merchandise identity.** Support a first-class Style, opaque internal SKU key and configured distinguishing attributes. Merchandise hierarchy levels and labels are configurable; financial/tax classification is separately governed. Transaction references use the internal identity, never an external code as the permanent key.

**R-MDM-002 — Alias lifecycle.** Support issuer/type-scoped, effective-dated many-to-one aliases for barcodes/EANs, brand articles and vendor codes. Preserve source mappings and reviewed equivalence. Once transactions exist, an identity cannot be silently replaced or merged to accommodate a changed barcode. Merging a duplicate vendor or brand master is a logged office action that names the surviving record; ledgers and open documents re-point to it, and nothing is silently deleted.

**R-MDM-003 — Ambiguity handling.** Intake and scan must surface conflicting aliases. Resolve through appropriate issuer/date/site/product context or governed confirmation; never select the first/latest match by default. Unknown and ambiguous identities remain distinguishable and require approved treatment.

**R-MDM-004 — Attributes and SKU profile.** Support configured fashion attributes such as brand, style/design, category, season, collection, gender, fit, size, colour and tags. The approved tenant profile defines which attributes distinguish an SKU; a fashion profile may use Style + Size + Colour. Missing attributes remain unknown unless an explicit approved default applies. Size supports approved numeric, alpha, age, cup, measurement or other text types.

**R-MDM-005 — Accountable quantities and physical identity.** Quantity two represents two accountable pieces even when the barcode is shared. Track quantity by identity, origin and location. Optional reliable unit identifiers are retained; mandatory per-piece serial tags are not required and FIFO lineage must not be portrayed as proof of the exact piece's journey.

**R-MDM-006 — Profile configuration.** Version supported canonical columns, labels/order, types, requiredness, vocabularies, aliases, defaults and supplied/selected/inherited/looked-up/derived field modes. Govern dependencies, rounding, overrides, currency/unit and table/export visibility. Identity, quantity/unit, location, origin and valuation meaning cannot be removed.

**R-MDM-007 — Profile approval and calculations.** Authorised preparers select supported versioned formats. Changed draft profiles are explicit corrections and invalidate affected approvals. Uploaded derived cells are cross-checks, not silently promoted inputs. Base-to-ticket and ticket-to-purchase directions require pinned approved formulas; unrestricted formulas and simultaneous multi-currency accounting are decision-gated by OQ-27.

**R-MDM-008 — Frozen evidence versus changing masters.** Receipt cost, physical ticket MRP, tax evidence and agreement references are frozen when the PT is approved and becomes official. Effective-dated master changes never recost old layers or change official exports. Receipt cost, carrying value, ticket MRP, selling price, transfer taxable value and settlement value are separate concepts.

**R-MDM-009 — Receipt costing calculation directions.** R-MDM-007's pinned approved formulas are stated here; the complete golden calculation cases are in Appendix C. With `t` = transport %, `g` = GST % of the selected slab and `m` = pricing margin %, as fractions, a receipt or opening line uses one of three directions: (a) **Base-to-ticket** — the preparer supplies BASIC (ex-tax cost), `t` and `m`; the system derives `P RATE = round_half_up_paise(BASIC × (1 + t))`, selects the HSN's applicable GST slab and derives `MRP = round_half_up_rupee(P RATE × (1 + g) ÷ (1 − m))`. (b) **Ticket-to-purchase** — the preparer supplies MRP; the applicable slab is the one MRP falls in; the system derives `P RATE = round_half_up_paise(MRP ÷ (1 + g) × (1 − m))` with no intermediate rounding, and `BASIC = P RATE`. (c) **Both-supplied** — the preparer supplies BASIC and MRP; the system derives `P RATE = round_half_up_paise(BASIC × (1 + t))` and keeps the supplied MRP unchanged. Opening lines using a supplied cost basis follow the both-supplied direction. Displayed MARGIN is a view, never an input: `round_half_up_2dp((MRP − P RATE) ÷ MRP × 100)`. P RATE is the layer cost. **Slab selection (base-to-ticket):** work out a candidate MRP under every GST slab configured for the line's HSN, keep the slabs whose candidate MRP falls inside that slab's own configured range, and pick the lowest resulting MRP if more than one qualifies; if none qualifies, the row cannot be resolved automatically and the preparer must supply an MRP inside a slab, after which the row follows the both-supplied direction and is marked for review. With a supplied MRP the applicable slab is the one that MRP falls in. **Validation bounds:** a receipt or opening line needs P RATE > 0, MRP > 0 and P RATE ≤ MRP; money values stay within ₹0.00–₹999,999,999.99, quantity within 1–999,999, and configured percentages (transport, margin, GST) within 0.00–99.99. A row outside any bound cannot be submitted, and a percentage-bound violation is refused before any division is attempted. Where an uploaded or pasted derived value (such as an external file's own P RATE) is supplied as a cross-check, it is compared with the computed value within a configured tolerance in minor currency units: within tolerance the row is accepted; outside it the row is blocked for review with the supplied value kept as evidence, and the computed value is never silently replaced. A transfer line carries its origin's frozen cost and MRP unchanged; no purchase formula runs on a transfer. All rounding is half-up at the stage and scale stated. Rates, slabs and HSN codes used in the golden cases are test inputs, not tenant defaults or tax advice; no formula or rate becomes effective for a tenant without its own approved scoped version.

**R-MDM-010 — Guided configuration and identity capture.** Product/configuration setup is guided in this order: vocabulary → SKU identity → rates/tax → PT columns/direction → preview → approval. Individual SKU entry and a size/colour combination-grid entry are both supported against the approved vocabulary; a user explicitly chooses which combinations exist, reviews them and creates them, with existing combinations and failures shown, and retried creation never duplicates what already succeeded. A standalone barcode lookup may show candidate matches but cannot itself record a contextual identity choice: a chosen identity is persisted only against a specific PT revision or scan event, and a reusable mapping is created only through the governed alias/crosswalk workflow, never an unbound pick. A master/configuration exception is raised only when a change creates actionable work: missing or invalid required setup routes to the site-readiness/owner queue, and unresolved master, crosswalk or identity work routes to the product master owner; ordinary versioning, retirement, withdrawal or a retained historic reference does not by itself create an alert. An approval read always carries the exact subject revision and a stable parent-document reference (document identity, kind, number, revision) where one exists; a screen never matches an approval by title text or reconstructs a revision from another read. Effective governed vocabulary can be read independently of an existing profile, so a first profile may select a restricted subset; an explicitly empty allowed-values list keeps its meaning of "unrestricted" and is not the only valid first choice.

**R-MDM-011 — House season calendar and brand-season mapping.** Season is a governed house calendar, not free-text codes. Each season follows the lifecycle Open → EOSS/markdown → Closed, and opening or closing a season is a logged office action. Warehouse and store users pick only Open seasons and never create one. A tenant that runs a two-season year may use halves. Margin bands are data held on the season, not a planning tool. Brand season labels (a season code, a blank or a date) map into the house season; the mapping is master-data work. A receipt requires a real mapped season (R-PT-005), and a PT row whose season is not on the house calendar is refused. A buying or merchandise planning calendar (open-to-buy, buying windows, appointments) is not part of this record. Where a receipt line has a booking, its season must match the booking's season, and where the approved profile constrains season it must match that too; a mismatch is a hard block, and the line cannot be approved until the season matches (Section 15.2, Receipt season match).

**Delivery boundary: Decision-gated.** Supported merchandise profiles, identity attributes and migration rules are approved through configuration; the stable-identity and frozen-evidence controls remain mandatory.

### 7.4 Booking, sourcing and procurement

**Default metadata:** Owner Buying / merchandising; priority Required; status Included unless overridden. Processes BP-02, BP-03, BP-11 and BP-15.

**R-BUY-001 — Booking intent.** A booking names exactly one vendor, brand and season. Each line requires style code and quantity; size and other attributes follow the approved booking profile and missing values remain unknown. “Free Size” must be explicitly supplied or confirmed as the actual size; omission never implies it. Historical values are not automatically reinterpreted. Colour, resolved stock SKU and indicative price/MRP are not prerequisites; binding cost and ticket evidence come from PT. A booking line holds style code, size, description, quantity, cost per piece and MRP; the booking shows total pieces, total cost and total MRP value, worked out from its lines. Cost per piece on a booking line is optional and indicative only. A booking's destination site is optional: a booking may be saved and confirmed carrying only its legal entity. While it has no destination site, only readers whose grant reaches it without needing a site (tenant-, brand- or entity-scoped for that legal entity) may see it; a site- or SBU-scoped reader, such as a store receiver, does not, and no read access is widened by this. Where a destination is named, it is on the header or on every applicable line; the product never guesses a default site. Once the buyer first names a destination, on a draft edit or a correction, that site becomes the booking's site permanently, in the same way as its booking number. Goods may be received against a booking only once it has a destination site. A buyer may read the effective governed values (including size and colour) required by the approved booking profile without gaining configuration-draft or configuration-approval authority.

**R-BUY-002 — Delivery progress.** Support many deliveries per booking and cumulative valid receipts/reversals against original booked quantities. Keep invoice-versus-count comparison shipment-specific. Allow over-receipt with remark; nothing auto-closes a booking. Explicit short-close records actor/reason and preserves original quantity. Receipts are linked to booking lines through a dated receipt-link action that keeps its actual date and actor. When a GRN is issued for goods received against a booking, each counted line that matches exactly one booking line (the same style and size) is linked to it at once through that same action; every other counted line stays unlinked for the buyer to link or resolve by hand, and nothing is guessed.

**R-BUY-003 — Unbooked and later-linked arrivals.** Permit arrival without a booking while retaining available agreement evidence. A later link is dated; it cannot rewrite actual counts, receipt cost or official PT evidence. A missing booking cannot trigger fabricated commercial defaults.

**R-BUY-004 — Operational/commercial separation.** Preserve the starting agreement and any evidence of title while using one receiving/count/transfer/selling workflow. SOR, buy-and-sell, correction, consignment, outright and V-stock labels do not independently determine ownership or liabilities. SIS/concession is not automatically a booking model. All goods in the tenant's network use one stock pool and one receiving, transfer, count and selling workflow; store and warehouse staff see them as the tenant's own stock regardless of the underlying commercial arrangement recorded on the booking. This does not settle contractual title, financial recognition or supplier liability; those remain governed by the approved commercial-model decisions. Commercial definitions remain OQ-26. **Status: Decision-gated for new commercial behaviour.**

**R-BUY-005 — Terms and purchase order lifecycle.** Support reusable credit-day, freight, discount, rebate and return-quota terms and frozen terms on approved POs. Intended lifecycle: create → approval by authority → transmit → vendor acknowledge → receive → PO/GRN/invoice matching → close/short-close. It is not an extra compulsory document before ordinary booking/receipt. **Status: Included, decision-gated.**

**R-BUY-006 — Later commercial changes.** Retain dated allowances, support expiry, credit notes, funding and approved commercial-status changes linked to originals. Never change original PT to apply new commercial terms. Settlement, buy-out, commission, payment and additional formulas require dedicated approval. **Status: Included, decision-gated.**

**R-BUY-007 — AI-assisted booking intake.** Accept supported booking documents and use AI-assisted extraction to create a structured booking draft with source provenance and confidence. The buyer reviews/corrects the draft before authorised confirmation saves the booking. AI cannot create an approved booking directly; manual entry remains complete. **Status: Included, decision-gated. See OQ-16/OQ-21.**

**R-BUY-008 — Communicated return windows.** Retain each original brand/vendor return notice and use AI-assisted extraction to create a reviewable draft of effective dates, eligible merchandise, destination and conditions. Once approved, derive eligible unsold stock, configurable deadline alerts and return lists. The user does not freeze a return window manually on each receipt/PT line. A changed notice creates a new effective version and never rewrites completed returns. No stock moves without the normal approved RTV workflow. Good unsold stock selected for a seasonal brand/vendor return is not quarantined merely because it will go back to a brand or vendor. **Status: Included, decision-gated. See OQ-48.**

**R-BUY-009 — Agent.** The party hierarchy is Vendor → Brand → optional Sub-brand, plus Agent. For non-branded sourcing, support Agent as a simple party record distinct from Vendor and Brand: name, city and the non-branded vendors it covers. An agent is linkable on a booking as the chase contact for payment, shipment and defect/quantity fixes. An agent is never a stock location and never the legal/commercial counterparty; the vendor remains that. What an agent guarantees or is liable for (payment, returns) is not modelled in this record and remains open. **See OQ-23.**

**Delivery boundary: Decision-gated.** Configuration selects the booking/PO/commercial/return capabilities that are activated, and the applicable Section 15 decisions approve their detailed authority, UX and acceptance criteria.

### 7.5 Physical receipt and canonical PT

**Default metadata:** Owner Warehouse / inventory control; priority Critical; status Included. Processes BP-03–BP-06 and BP-14.

**R-REC-001 — Actual arrival.** Capture arrival site, actual event time, recording time and counter/data-entry actors. The GRN arrival site is authoritative, even when it differs from booking destination or preparer workplace. An authorised warehouse preparer can work on a store GRN without inventing a warehouse receipt.

**R-REC-002 — Physical count.** Count by style code plus size at minimum, using USB HID scanner as the primary input with typed entry available. Prefill booking expectations where present. Count records physical quantity/condition only; good stock enters receiving hold and damaged stock is separately identifiable in quarantine. A PT is not required to report damage: if damage is found during receipt at a warehouse or store, record the quantity that physically arrived truthfully, mark the affected quantity as damaged and put it into quarantine at the receiving site immediately; damage does not reduce or hide the physical received count.

**R-REC-003 — Count effects and discrepancies.** GRN does not establish cost layers, value, tax, vendor liability or return allowance. Compare invoice claim with that shipment's count, require shortage/excess remark, and keep booking fulfilment separate. Wrong/rejected articles create typed owner/SLA exceptions with approved resolution routes, typically RTV. Damage does not prevent recording an honest count. Invoice-line matching to product uses the resolved SKU where known, otherwise a case-insensitive normalised description; genuine ambiguity is left for manual resolution, not guessed. The GRN records actual arrival truthfully: wrong means goods differ from what was expected, retaining the description and identity evidence actually available; excess means quantity exceeds what was expected; damage is a separate condition and may coexist with either discrepancy. Wrong and unidentified goods enter quarantine because they cannot join sellable stock safely; they retain the reason "wrong" or "unidentified" and are not falsely marked damaged. Correct goods also await PT after GRN, so a missing PT alone does not make goods wrong or unidentified; missing identity remains visible and no identity is invented. Wrong or excess goods going back use the RTV route against GRN custody without requiring a PT or completion of stock details solely for the return, preserving received quantity and observed condition. Acceptance of goods that are also damaged does not make them sellable or eligible for an ordinary good-stock PT; they retain their damage hold and follow the damaged-goods valuation route.

**R-REC-004 — Counter-GRN.** Correct counted evidence through authorised counter-GRN and linked subsequent actions. No silent edit/delete is allowed. A correction must respect already official PTs, allocations and downstream use rather than leaving layers inconsistent.

**R-REC-005 — Discrepancy disposition.** Retain supplier claims and actual count/condition separately. An authorised shortage disposition permits valid received quantities to obtain PT coverage while an owned vendor claim or reconciliation exception remains open. Excess awaiting acceptance remains in non-sellable excess hold; damaged and wrong/unidentified goods remain in quarantine. Later inventory acceptance requires resolved identity and approved cost/tax evidence, with no implicit copied cost or invented SKU. Accepted damaged stock may be valued but remains non-sellable. Rejection, return or disposal before PT coverage is recorded against held GRN quantity/evidence without inventing a cost layer or accounting effect. Configure authority by discrepancy type, quantity/value threshold, site and brand; retain reviewed revision, actor, reason, evidence, quantity and follow-up owner, with maker/checker separation where required. Missing authority blocks the affected quantity; an authorised hold may account for it while clean quantities proceed. Inventory acceptance for valuation is distinct from physical scan/putaway acceptance for availability. A reported damage requires a description and supporting evidence (such as a photograph or a signed inspection report) linked to the affected goods; existing GRN evidence already covering those goods may be reused, so a fresh upload is not compulsory and photographs are not the only acceptable format. Source value/tax evidence for the checker's decision is identified separately from the physical-damage evidence. A damaged-receipt scan always targets the site's own quarantine location; a client-supplied location cannot override it.

**R-REC-006 — Arrivals, count handover and duplicate warnings.** A delivery covering several invoices or brands is recorded as separate arrival records per vendor/brand/invoice combination; they may share one transporter reference and appear together, but each arrival keeps its own count session and GRN. One arrival has exactly one count session and one issued GRN; corrections after issue use counter-GRN evidence, never a second count or GRN. An unfinished count may be explicitly handed over to another authorised site person: original observation actors are retained, the handover itself is recorded, and later observations name their real actor; this continues the same count/GRN rather than opening a second receipt. A separately entered arrival sharing the same vendor/invoice within the receiving legal entity is warned, showing only records the recorder could otherwise see, and requires a stated reason (such as remaining cartons) to proceed. This business duplicate warning is distinct from command-replay/idempotency protection, and each count still covers only the goods physically in front of the counter. Growing reference pickers (entity, registration and similar) search and page; a one-page limit is not acceptable delivered behaviour. The count screen resolves a scanned or typed identity through the governed identity lookup; a genuine ambiguity requires an authorised choice bound to that scan, and genuinely unknown goods may remain unidentified with their description and condition recorded instead. An unfinished count whose holder is no longer available is reassigned, never cancelled: the Owner or Warehouse role reassigns it to another authorised person at the site, recording the reason, actor and time, and the same count and GRN continue. Any authorised receiver at the site may record observations into an open count, each naming its real actor (Section 15.2, Count handover).

**R-PT-001 — Canonical preparation paths.** Support AI-assisted ingestion of external PT, invoice and other source documents into canonical drafts with source provenance and confidence where applicable. Configuration defines the supported formats and conversion methods for this capability. Direct canonical upload and typed/pasted entry remain complete supported paths. Every path applies canonical validation, and the preparer reviews every draft before submission. Preparation without a brand PT is complete using invoice/vendor information; preserve valid physical barcodes and inherit known invoice, booking, vendor, brand, optional sub-brand and actual-site context. A shipment is branded only when all three hold: invoice present, vendor/brand barcode on every item, and a retrievable vendor PT. Missing any one keeps it on the untagged path: the preparer builds the canonical PT from the invoice, a vendor file or typed entry; the product then generates one barcode per resolved identity, shared by interchangeable pieces, for pieces that carry no valid barcode; and those pieces are printed and physically tagged before putaway. Pieces on the untagged path that already carry a valid physical barcode keep it: the barcode is recorded as an alias of the resolved identity and no replacement tag is printed. On the untagged path, MRP is sourced in this order: the vendor file if present; else the physical tag; else the approved standing formula (base + transport % + tax + margin band), which is tenant-configured, never hardcoded. A tag MRP already on the physical item is never overwritten. Existing vendor/brand barcodes on branded goods are kept and are not re-barcoded. **See OQ-16/OQ-21.**

**R-PT-002 — Original and conversion evidence.** Retain source file bytes/hash, source identity (including whether the draft was converted from a supplied brand PT file or prepared without one from invoice/vendor information), conversion linkage, manual source references and input revisions. Both preparation paths produce the same canonical stock representation and do not create a different transfer or selling workflow. Unsupported or structurally invalid intake is refused as a whole with a typed reason. Invalid mandatory rows never partially post. Preparation review remains separate from designated PT approval.

**R-PT-003 — Document cardinality and purposes.** Each GRN has at most one primary receipt PT and may have supplemental receipt PTs for previously uncovered quantities accepted later. Each PT has its own official number and at most one live official version. Enforce non-overlapping live quantity coverage across primary, supplemental and reissued PTs under concurrent approval. A supplement cannot revise previously covered quantity or cost; those corrections use safe reversal/reissue or linked corrective evidence. Opening PT uses migration context rather than GRN. Transfer PT derives from registered stock; destination receives against that same document, not a new vendor receipt.

**R-PT-004 — Lifecycle and review.** Lifecycle is Draft → Submitted → Approved/Official → Reversed. The preparer reviews and corrects the draft before submission. The designated approver acts on the submitted revision. Rejection by the approver or authorised withdrawal by the preparer returns the exact submitted revision to Draft with actor, reason and time retained; rejection and withdrawal are review events, not official PT states. Approval and officialisation are the same atomic event. Capture preparation mode, originals, calculated results, profile/calculation versions, currency/unit/rounding, agreement snapshots and origins. Before approval, changed inputs return the PT to draft/correction and invalidate the prior submission. After approval, official content never changes; corrections use safe reversal/reissue or linked corrective evidence under R-PT-009. Where preparing a PT proposes a new item (no existing item matches a row's identity-defining values), the product master owner confirms or rejects it separately from PT approval; a PT cannot be submitted while any of its rows still carries an unconfirmed new item.

**R-PT-005 — Validation, reconciliation and approval.** Validate required fields, identity, quantity/unit, approved profile meaning, season treatment and cost ≤ MRP for the submitted quantities. Receipt requires a real mapped season. Where the receipt line has a booking, its season must match the booking's season, and where the approved profile constrains season it must match that too; a mismatch is a hard block, and the line cannot be approved until the season matches (Section 15.2, Receipt season match). Migration assigns each opening row its real season from the item's own data. An opening row may instead carry the explicit value *unknown historical season* only where no season can be found for that item (Section 15.2, Opening stock): a first-class value, searchable, filterable, reportable and shown as such wherever season is shown. It is never inferred, defaulted or derived; the person loading the manifest chooses it deliberately, and the choice is audited with actor and time. Stock carrying it matches no season-specific offer; season-free offers still apply. It loosens no other required field: identity, quantity, location and valuation evidence remain mandatory on every opening row, and a row missing any of them is rejected with the reason. If the real season is later established, the correction is a new governed event recording who, when and why; the original manifest row, its verification and any bill, transfer or label already issued against the unknown-season stock keep their original snapshot. Opening stock carrying this value still creates no vendor delivery, booking, invoice or duplicate purchase liability; it remains not a receipt. Opening-stock activation for any non-synthetic tenant remains blocked until its migration sources are recorded (OQ-29; Section 15.2.1 rule 7). Every approval attempt performs purpose-specific reconciliation: receipt compares invoice, GRN, existing coverage and proposed PT with authorised discrepancy dispositions; opening compares the approved manifest and required physical verification; transfer validates eligible existing source allocations and preserved receipt evidence. Unexplained differences, duplicate coverage, invalid submitted rows and missing required cost/MRP, prohibited zero values or unresolved identity ambiguity block approval. An authorised hold on excluded GRN quantities does not block clean submitted quantities. Do not change truthful source records simply to force equal totals. Whichever supported path fills a receipt PT (starting from the GRN's own count, a canonical-layout upload or a mapped brand file), it still reconciles to that GRN's counted lots; a receipt PT is never a blank document prepared with no GRN behind it. Product default is one designated approval gate with configured checks and authority. The default approver is the Owner role, who must be a different person from the preparer. Configuration lets the administrator choose office (Owner role), warehouse (Warehouse role) or either as the approving side, plus an optional second check in which the warehouse checks the file and the office approves. Whichever setting is active, the preparer never approves their own PT. An approval is refused as self-approval not only when the approver is the document's preparer, but also when the approver is the document's maker or anyone who reviewed its rows during preparation. The full role/action matrix remains OQ-28.

**R-PT-006 — Atomic approval and officialisation.** Every PT approval validates authority and its purpose-specific reconciliation, freezes official content and identity/alias/ticket/tax/value evidence, issues the official number/version and records audit/idempotency evidence with its stock effects as one all-or-nothing result. Primary/supplemental receipt approval is the sole new receipt-value writer and opens layers for previously uncovered accepted quantities without adding GRN count again. Opening approval creates opening quantity and operational layers from the approved manifest without purchase or books effects. For receipt/opening, official line values equal newly opened layer value. Transfer approval reserves existing eligible source allocations and creates no new inbound value; dispatch and destination receipt remain separate atomic events preserving original lineage. Approval alone never releases receiving hold or overrides physical acceptance/condition restrictions.

**R-PT-007 — Approval evidence.** Every approval attempt records actor, submitted revision, reconciliation result, applicable values/thresholds, rule version and outcome. Changed inputs cannot inherit stale approval. Repeat approval returns the original result rather than issuing a second number, receipt or value posting.

**R-PT-008 — Official evidence protection.** Official header, rows, original quantities, inputs/results, versions and source evidence cannot be altered through normal, bulk, API, administrative or migration paths. Counter/corrective evidence is new and linked. Technical prevention and privileged bypass handling must be proved; the database brand is not proof of immutability. **See OQ-33.**

**R-PT-009 — Reversal and reissue.** Reverse only where downstream state makes it safe, counter actual evidence and retain refusals. Reissue retains original origins and document number with the next approved official version, but receives fresh approvals. Historic identifiers and old receipt costs are never renumbered or rewritten. A safe receipt-PT reversal returns put-away good quantity to receiving and non-good quantity to quarantine; quantity already held stays in its required hold.

**R-PT-010 — PT presentation and download.** Provide an authorised in-app table with context/history and Excel `.xlsx` export using the frozen profile. A download is read-only: no stock event, no reinterpretation under current masters and no substitution of current remaining balance for original row quantity.

**R-PT-011 — Size and failure contract.** Publish supported PT line/file/evidence limits for each approved operating profile. Validate before posting and retain actionable refusal/retry status. A platform limit cannot justify a partially posted receipt or multiple separately committed operations being called one atomic approval. **Status: Foundation safeguard. See OQ-34.**

**R-PT-012 — PT history depth.** A PT's detail history is paginated independently of its lines: newest event first, 50 events per page, including draft corrections, submission/withdrawal, approval/refusal, official versions/reissues, reversals and related postings, each with authorised actor, time, revision, outcome, reason and evidence. An unpaginated or empty history view is not acceptable delivered behaviour.

**R-PT-013 — Preparation review discipline.** A preparer may review individual rows and explicitly review a selected set of rows on the current page; there is no whole-file "mark everything reviewed" shortcut. Reviewed/total progress is shown, and a changed input clears the review mark(s) it affects. Saving a draft is explicit ("Save changes"), shows an unsaved indicator and warns before leaving the screen; saving neither reviews nor submits the draft. If another person changes the revision while a preparer is mid-edit, the preparer's conflicting local edits are preserved for comparison and a fresh review is required after refetching. A receipt PT stays bound to its GRN: the direct typed/pasted preparation path starts from that GRN's counted lots and lets the preparer type or paste canonical cell values into the resulting grid; it is never a blank receipt document with no GRN parent. PT preparers and approvers may read the effective profile needed for their work without this by itself granting access to rate or tax payloads, which require their own field authority. A reissue control is shown only when the system's allowed-actions answer grants reissue to that person for that PT's purpose; receipt reissue belongs to the preparer surface and approval remains a distinct checker action. A control known to be refused is never shown.

**R-PT-014 — Tags, labels and print evidence.** Generate scannable tag/label output from frozen official PT-line values (identity, size, MRP). A printed label carries the frozen official PT line's own values, never a current or projected value. A physical barcode symbol carries only the frozen printable alias; human-readable label text carries MRP and other approved display fields. Every print or reprint is a governed, audited job recording quantity, actor and time; a reprint does not silently replace the original job's evidence. Label evidence is derived by the system from the selected official line and its frozen source or print job; a user never types an opaque evidence identifier, and internal PT identifiers, candidate hashes and evidence identifiers are never encoded into the barcode itself. Scan label evidence accepted during acceptance is limited to the PT line's own print job, the readable PT source file used for vendor tags, or, for a typed preparation with no file, the approved PT version itself; acceptance scanning cites the label's own record as its evidence, not a re-derived value. A receiving or acceptance scan must verify the tag against its originating official PT line and MRP before it is treated as identifying that stock. Where a print run is only partly successful, the operator confirms the usable count per line while the original requested counts are preserved; a reprint covers only the missing quantity, through a new linked job carrying its own reason. An uncertain usable quantity is recorded as unknown and must be checked before any reprint, and a partially successful job is never automatically repeated in full. A browser print dialog or print-completion event proves an attempt was made, not that the labels physically printed; scanning a sample proves that sample, not that every requested copy printed.

**Delivery boundary: Decision-gated.** Configuration defines the supported source formats and preparation paths, reconciliation detail, roles, limits and acceptance tests. Human approval, atomic officialisation and immutable official evidence remain mandatory.

### 7.6 Receipt layers, movement and inventory control

**Default metadata:** Owner Inventory control; priority Critical; status Included. Processes BP-05–BP-08 and BP-14–BP-16.

**R-INV-001 — Operational layers.** Each approved receipt or opening line establishes quantity, frozen cost, ticket MRP, required season treatment, applicable source time and original evidence under its purpose-specific profile. Receipt layers retain actual arrival time; opening layers retain historical origin or receipt time where known and the cutover context separately. Unknown history remains unknown unless the approved opening profile requires a value before approval. Registered stock retains layer lineage through movement. Explicit unvalued/unavailable pre-PT states include receiving hold, excess hold and quarantine.

**R-INV-002 — FIFO and sale eligibility.** Sales consume eligible site/SKU receipt layers FIFO and never sell before official PT plus physical acceptance. Cost-pending sale lines are prohibited. Mixed held/accepted origins of the same SKU are checked separately. Independent offline allocation and oversell must satisfy the approved OQ-30 policy; this document does not invent a resolution.

**R-INV-003 — Stock availability.** ATS requires official PT covering the actual store, accepted good quantity in floor/backstore and no applicable hold/reservation. Transit is not destination on-hand. In-transit rows are reported at the source site, showing the transfer, source, destination and transit quantity; a physical stock value summary excludes transit and reports it separately. Stock-state dimensions must not double-subtract overlapping holds. Generic moves, adjustments and transfers cannot bypass acceptance. ATS is a store concept: a warehouse site shows no sellable (ATS) quantity, only transferable quantity. Sellable and transferable quantities both require a resolved SKU; unidentified stock is neither.

**R-INV-004 — Transfer derivation.** Every internal warehouse/store hop, including same-state movement, has a derived transfer PT with destination, selected quantities and original receipt allocations. Source must be eligible, accepted and unreserved at allocation time. Transfer cannot exceed current source availability or reapply purchase markup. Separate origins remain separate even within one SKU/document. Routes map to movement documents as follows: warehouse↔store, store↔store and warehouse↔warehouse are internal transfers; store or warehouse → vendor/brand, including direct vendor pickup, is RTV; store → linked warehouse → vendor/brand is an internal transfer followed by a separate RTV. Internal transfers and RTV remain distinct document types; shared physical execution (a dispatch, a pickup) does not turn an RTV into an internal stock transfer. One transfer identifies one source and one destination; separate destinations require separate transfers. **Held-goods custody exception:** quarantined goods, including quarantined goods not yet covered by an official PT, may move between sites and keep their hold throughout — for example store quarantine → warehouse quarantine as an internal transfer preserving condition and source references, and store or warehouse quarantine → vendor/brand through RTV. Such a movement uses only the GRN/custody information actually available; it does not make the goods ordinary eligible stock, does not release or alter their hold, valuation state or eligibility, and does not invent identity, cost, tax or stock-layer detail to enable the move. Moving quarantined goods never makes them sellable or allocatable as ordinary stock, and destination receipt retains their quarantine condition. Confirmed damaged goods may also remain in store or warehouse quarantine indefinitely; movement is optional.

**R-INV-005 — Transfer milestones.** Creation, approval/reservation, dispatch, actual arrival, scan acceptance and putaway are separately recorded. Reservation removes source availability; dispatch moves to transit. Electronic destination visibility creates a pending receipt only. Dispatch and receipt are separate atomic, duplicate-safe events with actual and recorded time. E-way evidence for the movement follows R-FIN-018/R-FIN-019; physical completion does not silently close a documentation exception, and the internal transfer PT and statutory movement evidence remain separate linked documents.

**R-INV-006 — Destination receipt by whole dispatch.** Receive against the same official transfer PT and carry original cost/ticket evidence into destination stock. A transfer may be fulfilled partially through several dispatches, and multiple dispatches against the same approved transfer are received separately; each dispatch is received as one whole shipment through the destination's receiving flow, and there is no staged partial receipt against one dispatch. Goods not received in a dispatch are recorded as short on that dispatch's receipt; any remainder of the transfer moves only on a later, separate dispatch. Whole-dispatch receiving means accounting for the shipment, not asserting that all expected goods arrived correctly: preserve expected and actual received quantities and observed condition, and release only actual eligible accepted quantity under normal receiving rules. Treatment by observation: goods already quarantined at source keep their quarantine at the destination; damage discovered at arrival is recorded by the receiver as damaged and enters quarantine at the receiving site immediately, preserving the transfer and sending-site references, and is never recorded as shortage — where the damage actually happened may be unknown, so the system records where and when it was discovered without inventing fault; wrong or unidentified goods preserve actual custody/identity evidence and follow the quarantine-entry and outcome rules; excess goods are recorded separately with an unavailable hold pending the applicable acceptance/return process, with no invented source quantity, identity or value; shortage is recorded as an explicit discrepancy against the dispatch, and absent goods do not enter quarantine. Short and damaged rows on a transfer arrival are typed exception rows, never free text, valued at receipt-time book value. Unreceived goods stay pending/transit or follow explicit short, excess, wrong or damaged resolution. An unfinished scan session cannot auto-short-close: it does not automatically declare missing goods short or complete the dispatch. Applying the wrong/excess acceptance and outcome rules to a transfer arrival does not turn it into a new supplier GRN or purchase; the transfer source and receiving evidence are preserved. Eligible good goods can become available independently of disputed quantities; recording receipt does not automatically settle or erase a discrepancy, and missing or disputed quantities require explicit linked resolution before they count as accounted for at final movement closure.

**R-INV-007 — Conserved, branched history.** Preserve original arrival and receipt origins through repeated hops, branch destinations and returns. A destination display cannot replace origin. Example: a 30-piece receipt with ten shirts, ten pants and ten T-shirts remains 30 on its official PT after a 15-piece transfer of five each; derived documents establish balances. Original receipt quantities, identity, condition and separate cost origins are preserved through every hop; a transfer creates no new purchase value, and grouped display rows must not discard separate origins. Excess on a transfer never increases the original reservation.

**R-INV-008 — Controlled movement types.** Support receipt, opening, RTV, inter-site transfer, within-site bin/location move, adjustment up/down, write-off, disposal and shrinkage with reason, actor, number and time. Write-off and disposal follow R-INV-022. Floor/backstore moves are not inter-site transfers. Sales, returns and corrective documents retain canonical stock references and value effects where approved.

**R-INV-009 — Counts and stock exceptions.** Support blind cycle counts and full physical inventory with counter identity, original observations, variance and governed adjustment. A count applies the observed variance as a delta against the current book position at its frozen as-of point; it never overwrites book stock with the raw observed total. A count session with zero variance closes without posting a movement. Cancelling a count retains its observations as evidence and releases the count's freeze on the counted stock. At a trading store, starting a count requires online confirmation that all tills have synced and stopped finalising sales; sales at that store stay frozen until the count closes or is cancelled and the freeze is released (OQ-57, answered 29 September 2026). Offline oversell differences surface as owned negative-stock exceptions without rewriting bills; they are not permission for online over-allocation or bypassing held origins. Commercial return quotas remain gated by approved policy.

**R-INV-010 — Opening-stock PT.** Opening represents stock already present at cutover. Retain manifest/batch/row, site/bin/condition, identity, quantity/unit, canonical value evidence and older origins where known. Use the approved supplied or derived cost basis, MRP/ticket and tax evidence defined by the pinned opening profile. Never invent GRN, booking, purchase, owner/title, payable, journal, tax claim or return allowance. **See OQ-29.**

**R-INV-011 — Opening validation and acceptance.** Apply the opening-specific atomic approval contract, immutable evidence, safe reversal/reissue and duplicate/concurrent-retry protection. Reconcile the proposed PT to the approved manifest and required physical verification by site, SKU/unit, condition and value basis. A draft correction or authorised opening-variance decision retains the original manifest, observations, accepted quantity/value, reason and approver; it cannot invent identity/cost evidence or change the pinned valuation formula. Only valid accepted quantities/values enter the submitted PT; excluded or unresolved manifest rows remain visible and any later opening batch requires approved source context and checks against prior openings. Evidenced accepted floor/backstore/bin status may be retained; valued held/damaged stock remains unavailable until applicable controls permit release. External-books preparation remains separate. **See OQ-29.**

**R-INV-012 — Reconciliation dimensions and trigger.** Keep booking-versus-cumulative-receipts separate from receipt reconciliation. Every receipt PT approval attempt compares invoice claims, actual GRN count, existing PT coverage and proposed coverage at line/total level. Account for all physical quantities in disjoint categories: covered by this PT, already covered by another live PT, held uncovered, or rejected/returned/disposed without coverage. Later movements of covered stock remain in its layer history rather than being counted again as uncovered dispositions. Invoice shortages concern goods never received and remain separate. Authorised discrepancy dispositions may preserve unequal source totals; unexplained differences and overlapping coverage block approval. Revalidate coverage atomically for concurrent primary/supplement/reissue attempts. Reversal counters live coverage without erasing history. Opening and transfer use their own reconciliation contracts under R-PT-005/006. Reconciliation cannot create a second stock effect or waive validity, conservation, physical custody or permissions. An invoice that arrives after a receipt PT has already covered every counted piece never reopens or blocks that PT: any resulting excess becomes an owned excess exception for the buyer, closed with a note and authority. A receipt PT never waits for an invoice (Section 15.2, Late-invoice excess).

**R-INV-013 — Return-quota evidence.** Where the approved commercial policy enables a brand-season return pool, track its ceiling, dated changes and consumption, and freeze the applicable quota reading on each affected document. A GRN or opening PT creates no return allowance by itself. The direction, eligibility and commercial effect of each consumption event remain governed by OQ-26/OQ-43 rather than inferred from a generic customer return. The RTV/seasonal-return money treatment (credit-due tracking, brand-owned quantity return with memo relief, over-cap conversion to company-owned stock) waits on the commercial-model decision in OQ-26.

**R-INV-014 — Billed-retained custody.** A finalised billed-retained sale removes the sold quantity from ATS and records that the customer-purchased goods remain physically in store custody. Retain customer, bill/line, SKU/origin, quantity, holding site/location, expected pickup/delivery and final handover, cancellation or return outcome. Billed-retained goods cannot be sold, allocated, transferred as retailer stock or returned to a brand/vendor. This is not a reservation or an unfinished bill. **Status: Included, decision-gated. See OQ-49.**

**R-INV-015 — Check-only and combined acceptance/putaway.** Physical acceptance supports "check only" and combined "check and put away". Acceptance starts in check-only; switching to check-and-putaway requires an explicit actual destination location, and checking alone never releases stock. A scan defaults to one piece; an explicit counted quantity may only group pieces that share the same official line, tag/MRP, condition and destination, and the entered quantity resets to one after each entry. This does not introduce mandatory per-piece serial identity. A person authorised to accept stock receives a narrow, site-scoped pending-acceptance read containing only enough official-PT reference and remaining-quantity information to open or resume acceptance; broader PT or exception read authority is never granted merely to let them discover this work. An over-scan, or a piece outside the official line, is not a fourth physical condition called "extra": it is refused from acceptance and routed back to the count/counter-GRN and identity/disposition flow, with any eligible corrected quantity later usable on a supplemental PT. The acceptance screen may guide that route but never invents an "extra" acceptance outcome. Completing acceptance while pieces remain unaccepted requires explicit confirmation; the remaining pieces stay visible and are not written off.

**R-INV-016 — Movement approval, reservation and dispatches.** Every internal transfer and RTV movement requires approval before dispatch; each leg of a multi-hop movement (for example store → warehouse → vendor) has its own approved movement. Approval reserves the approved quantities against source stock/custody and prevents the same quantity being assigned to a competing movement or sold while reserved. An undispatched balance remains reserved until dispatched or explicitly cancelled; there is no automatic expiry. Cancellation releases only the outstanding reservation, not an independent quarantine or other hold, and retains actor, time, quantity and reason. Changed items, an increased quantity or a different destination require fresh approval; completed dispatches and their evidence remain unchanged, and a smaller dispatch leaves the remaining quantity reserved unless explicitly cancelled. One approved transfer or RTV supports multiple dispatches or pickups. Each dispatch records its quantities, source references, actual departure time, recording time, actor and movement/document evidence, and belongs to the approved movement; cumulative dispatch cannot exceed the approved quantities or reuse a cancelled balance without renewed approval. Creation and approval do not record physical departure: reservation happens at approval, and actual dispatch separately records departure and transit. Electronic visibility at the destination is a pending receipt, not proof of arrival or available stock.

**R-INV-017 — Resumable dispatch scans.** Acknowledged dispatch-preparation scans are saved against the exact approved movement version, with operator and time, so an authorised source-site user can resume them after closing the screen or switching devices. Scanning alone never dispatches stock: final dispatch still requires the complete approved quantities plus fresh reservation, hold and stock checks at that moment. A cancellation or a replacement approved version makes earlier scan work unusable for dispatch while retaining it as evidence; no old scan pass silently authorises a new plan.

**R-INV-018 — Transit-shortage evidence.** Proposing a transit shortage requires the destination's own recorded check/recount, the exact missing transfer lines and quantities, and a reason with supporting evidence. Source/transporter follow-up is recorded, but no additional mandatory source sign-off is required beyond the distinct-checker approval; pending that approval, the missing quantity remains recorded as in transit.

**R-INV-019 — Physical completion and handover evidence.** Handover evidence may be vendor/receiving-entity evidence or pickup confirmation recorded by the responsible warehouse or store person; no single evidence format is mandated. Retain the confirming actor, event time, recording time, actual quantity and movement/dispatch reference, with supplied evidence; a dispatch record alone is not proof of vendor receipt. Physical completion by movement type: for an internal dispatch, the destination's receiving flow records the shipment and accounts for its quantities, including explicit discrepancy resolution; for a direct vendor pickup, confirmed handover to the vendor or its pickup representative accounts for the quantity collected; for dispatch for delivery to the vendor, vendor receipt must be confirmed and source departure alone is insufficient. Each dispatch completes individually. The overall transfer or RTV completes only when every dispatched quantity is accounted for through receipt, confirmed vendor pickup, recorded return-to-source or explicit discrepancy resolution, and no undispatched balance remains reserved. A returned or cancelled quantity is never labelled successfully delivered. Cancelling the undispatched balance does not close a dispatch still awaiting receipt, and a partially collected RTV can remain open for another pickup. Physical completion is separate from credit, replacement or financial settlement; a replacement is a linked inbound receipt, not a reversal erasing the original handover.

**R-INV-020 — Return deadlines and reminders.** When a return deadline is supplied against a damaged-goods pickup or other return, retain it against the relevant return and show pending action and reminders on the dashboards of the responsible higher authorities while a balance remains pending. A deadline or reminder does not dispatch, cancel or release stock automatically. Damaged-goods pickup does not acquire a mandatory deadline merely because reminders are supported. Commercial rules that calculate eligibility, establish a deadline or choose its qualifying event, and reminder timing and role mapping, remain open. **See OQ-48.**

**R-INV-021 — RTV lifecycle and vendor acknowledgement.** Creating an RTV does not remove goods from physical custody. RTV status is: *Initiated* — return recorded, and a balance awaits departure or a dispatched shipment has not completed; *Completed* — the full RTV quantity has completed under R-INV-019; *Closed — partially returned* — only part of the RTV quantity was returned, all dispatched quantities are accounted for and the entire undispatched balance was explicitly withdrawn; *Cancelled* — return cancelled before any goods left, and the goods remain in quarantine. After a partial departure, withdrawing the entire undispatched balance permits closure as partially returned only when the dispatched quantities are also accounted for; it does not undo departure or close a shipment still awaiting vendor receipt. Partial-pickup reasons are recorded against the quantities left behind, and one pickup may have several reasons covering different quantities: vendor rejected the goods — the quantity remains in quarantine and no further pickup is expected under this RTV; vendor will collect the balance later — the quantity remains assigned to the initiated RTV, with no mandatory deadline but any supplied deadline retained; goods were not ready for handover — the quantity remains assigned to the initiated RTV; return withdrawn by us — the quantity remains in quarantine and no further pickup is expected; other — a remark is required, including whether another pickup is expected. The RTV stays initiated while any balance still awaits departure or a dispatched shipment remains incomplete. Rejected quantities with no further pickup expected must be explicitly withdrawn from the pending balance; withdrawn quantities retain their RTV history and may receive a later separate disposition. Whether "cancel" (release of an undispatched reservation under R-INV-016) and "withdraw" (release of an RTV balance here) are one action or two distinct actions with different evidence or authority is OQ-62. Vendor-acknowledged quantities and evidence are recorded per RTV line; where fewer goods are acknowledged than were dispatched, the difference is kept as an owned RTV discrepancy rather than marking the whole RTV confirmed. The original dispatch record is preserved, and an acknowledgement mismatch by itself cannot restore stock, create another write-off or create a credit note. Which action, evidence, authority, deadline and business outcome close a persistent acknowledgement discrepancy remains open under OQ-56; meanwhile the open difference is captured safely and no generic close, automatic stock restoration, additional write-off or credit-note settlement is invented.

**R-INV-022 — Write-off and disposal.** Write-off records the decision to recognise loss of an established recorded value; it does not by itself record physical departure, so goods still physically present remain counted at their site, in quarantine and unavailable to sell. Disposal records the actual physical removal of goods from company custody through destruction or handover for scrap/recycling; it removes only the quantity actually disposed of, retaining its source and quarantine history. Write-off and disposal may occur together or separately; neither is automatic merely because goods are damaged, non-returnable or have remained in quarantine for a long time. Non-returnable damaged goods may remain in quarantine indefinitely. The warehouse does not write off goods on its own authority. Supported disposal methods are destruction and scrap/recycling handover; donation and sale as damaged merchandise are not supported until a Section 15 decision adds them. A disposal is a numbered record with site, affected goods/custody references, actual quantity, reason, method, actual event time, recording time, actor and evidence of destruction or handover. Partial disposal leaves the remaining quantity in quarantine. Quantity still committed to a pending RTV cannot be disposed of until it is first withdrawn from the RTV, preserving its history. Any scrap proceeds are recorded separately from the value lost. A write-off retains the affected quantity/source references, established value and its basis, reason, actor, time and link to disposal when applicable, and the same value loss cannot be recognised twice when physical disposal follows an earlier write-off. Goods disposed of before PT may have no established value: record disposal against GRN custody without inventing a cost layer, zero value, payable or accounting effect; unknown value remains unknown. Operational memo value is not automatically accounting value. This rule does not prescribe journal entries, tax treatment or rights to write off vendor-owned goods, which require their own approved decisions, and recording a disposal implies no financial posting. Completed events preserve history; corrections use explicit linked correction records rather than deleting the original event.

**R-INV-023 — Damage reporting and confirmation.** Goods may be marked damaged when damage or a defect is discovered while already held at a store or warehouse, including on the floor, in the backstore or warehouse, during handling, picking, packing or stocktake, and before sale; the affected quantity enters quarantine at the site where the damage is discovered. Any staff member authorised to handle stock at the site may report damage. Reporting immediately quarantines the affected quantity and makes it unavailable to sell, allocate, pick or transfer as ordinary stock. A different authorised person confirms or rejects the report; the reporter cannot confirm their own report. Confirmation preserves the reporter, confirmer, site, time, quantity, goods identity, source document where applicable, reason and evidence, and creates no second stock movement because the quantity was already quarantined when reported. Detailed role permissions follow the role/action matrix. **See OQ-28.**

**R-INV-024 — Count completeness confirmation.** Before submitting a count pass, the counter affirms having counted the whole assigned area/scope, including empty locations; this affirmation is bound to the submitted observation revision and the assigned scope, and never reveals expected quantities, so counts stay blind. An unfinished pass remains resumable rather than being submitted as complete. Because unscanned known stock is otherwise treated as zero observed, this affirmation guards against that consequence; it does not replace the required review and distinct approval that follow. A recount concerns only its explicitly assigned scope, never an invented whole-site recount.

**R-INV-025 — Historical stock-read limitations.** Every stock read declares, per read, which of a fixed set of historical-view limitations apply: whether site readiness, location kind and cost-record projections are shown as current, because no versioned history exists for them, rather than as they truly stood at the requested time. The set that applies is fixed per read type by what that read actually consults, not judged row by row or per caller; all applicable limitations apply to every past-dated read on a given surface, and none apply to a live (current-time) read. A read that consults only frozen, already-recorded addresses and quantities (such as a ledger/journal entry replaying its own posted content) declares fewer limitations than a read that re-derives current site or location context. A declared limitation never fills in an unknown value; an unvalued or incomplete figure stays unknown, never a substituted zero.

**R-INV-026 — Unavailable historical origin at opening.** Where an opening row's older origin cannot be established, an authorised owner action explicitly confirms "historical origin unavailable" with a reason and supporting evidence. This closes the investigation into that missing fact, not the fact itself: the origin stays visibly unknown, the product never invents a date/reference or edits official history to fill it, and a generic note is not accepted as this closure. An opening row whose verified physical condition is not good opens as unvalued quarantine custody at its site, retaining the physical lot and its duplicate claim, with no origin, PT coverage or value leg created for it. The opening-variance decision always carries the accepted condition, defaulting to the condition physical verification actually observed, so a quantity-only decision can never let a row verified as damaged open as good stock. **See OQ-29.**

**R-INV-027 — Allocation to linked stores.** A warehouse serves a configured set of linked own stores, and allocation goes only to that set; a destination outside it is a partner dispatch (R-PAR-005), not an allocation. The split happens after inward, from what actually arrived, never from the original order. Allocation rows carry style + size, destination site and quantity, tied to the shipment, brand and season, and the input used for the split (usually each store's past-sales share) is stored with the decision. Allocated quantity cannot exceed the remaining inwarded quantity available to allocate, excluding existing holds and unavailable stock. One inwarded receipt PT produces one transfer PT per destination that is not the arrival site; stock that stays at the arrival site uses the receipt PT itself, with no invented warehouse hop. Only an unshipped allocation may be re-split; quantity already picked or shipped cannot be silently moved and needs a new transfer or a reversal.

**Delivery boundary: Decision-gated.** Configuration selects the movement, acceptance, return and billed-retained capabilities that are activated, and defines their detailed UX and exception policies. Conservation, lineage and sale eligibility remain mandatory.

### 7.7 Pricing, offers and promotions

**Default metadata:** Owner Buying / pricing steward; priority Required; status Included and decision-gated. Processes BP-07 and BP-09.

**R-PRC-001 — Funding and authority.** Support brand, retailer and shared funding recorded on every discounted sale line. Brand circulars and AI suggestions create drafts only. The commercial funding/authority matrix must be approved before use; offer authority is not automatically derived from presumed legal stock ownership.

**R-PRC-002 — Offer types.** Support spend slabs, percentage-off including quantity tiers, buy-X-get-Y/cheapest-free, gift-with-purchase including fallback, fixed price by ticket/MRP band, flat money-off, end-of-season sale and clearance step-down. Each enabled type requires approved calculation, eligibility and tax treatment; free customer sale rewards do not imply zero receipt cost.

**R-PRC-003 — Rule definition.** Record trigger, reward, store/group/date/brand/category/SKU scope, exclusions, funding, priority, effective version and stacking permission. Retain no-discount styles/categories. Manual discount limits, final authority and permitted minimum-price behaviour require approved tenant policy; no universal percentage is assumed. The default configuration has no manual counter discount: all discounting at the till runs through approved offers. Manual counter discount remains a capability that tenant policy may enable within approved limits and authority; it is off in the initial configuration (Section 15.2).

**R-PRC-004 — Calculation and conflict rules.** The offer basis is the frozen official PT-line MRP (physical ticket MRP) by default, and the till sells from that frozen MRP; permitted exceptions, scope and versioning of the basis remain OQ-55. Approved offers apply at the till. When explicitly allowed, sequential discounts apply to already-discounted price and slab rewards allocate proportionally back to qualifying items with governed rounding. Permit one brand offer per item; store/bank stacking requires explicit permission. Stable priority/order resolves matches; no-discount overrides. Tax uses applicable dated configuration, not a hardcoded apparel ladder. The offer applicability shown for an item wherever running offers are listed agrees exactly with what checkout applies to it: one calculation answers both, and a listing is never a separate, potentially diverging calculation. **See OQ-55.**

**R-PRC-005 — Offer distribution and change.** Human confirms every rule before till activation. Record which stores/rule versions received an offer; new stores are not automatically enrolled. Offline tills do not apply offers delivered after disconnection. Parked carts crossing trading-date boundaries reprice under applicable available rules before finalisation.

**R-PRC-006 — Suggestions and margin controls.** Use age, velocity, broken sizes, season and approved margin floor to propose markdown or step-down. A suggestion must not breach the approved break-even floor; unavailable cost/funding inputs prevent a defensible floor calculation. Human approves action; dead-stock declaration after repeated unsuccessful offers exits the offer workflow. **Status: Included, decision-gated.**

**R-PRC-007 — Golden vectors.** Shared human-authored reference cases prove POS/server equivalence for slabs, cheapest-free, gift/fallback, no-discount skips, dates and stacking. Vectors are versioned immutable artifacts; revised expected outcomes require explicit approval and retention of earlier cases. Never regenerate expected answers from implementation to make a check pass. **Priority: Critical.**

**R-PRC-008 — Simulation and evaluation.** Simulate approved offers against historical sales and show estimated discount depth, margin impact and affected units, with source period and assumptions. Measure observed outcomes separately from simulated uplift. **Status: Included.**

**R-PRC-009 — EOSS and discount profitability.** For each activated EOSS or clearance period, show revenue, units, discount depth, stock depletion, gross margin, brand/vendor funding and claims under approved formulas. Provide low-margin and margin-floor alerts before approval and actual profitability after sale. Missing cost, commission, funding or allocation inputs are shown as incomplete and never replaced with invented values. **Status: Included, decision-gated.**

**Delivery boundary: Decision-gated.** Offers at the till on the ticket-MRP default basis (R-PRC-004) are decided; each enabled offer type still requires its approved calculation, eligibility, tax treatment and golden cases (R-PRC-002, R-PRC-007). Before any other pricing or EOSS implementation, OQ-20, the open parts of OQ-55 and applicable commercial questions must record approved formulas, authority, funding, UX and golden cases in this PRD; activation also requires the acceptance evidence.

### 7.8 Store operations and offline POS

**Default metadata:** Owner Store operations; priority Critical; status Included. The one-till offline defaults in R-POS-003 are decided; any other offline activation remains subject to OQ-30–OQ-32, OQ-35 and OQ-45. Processes BP-07 and BP-08.

**R-POS-001 — Supported till.** POS is a desktop/laptop browser application using scanner/keyboard workflows. A store-scoped local working set contains only authorised identities/aliases, eligible site/origin/quantity snapshots, ticket evidence, applicable pricing/offer/tax versions and permitted customer/bill lookups. The browser/device support matrix and local data confidentiality are explicit requirements. Specifically, the working set holds authorised item identities and aliases, current prices, applicable offers, tax versions, the store's own eligible-quantity snapshot and the original bills eligible for exchange at that store; it never holds cost, margin or receipt-origin value. It is versioned and issued per registered till. The cached-bill window for an offline exchange is a configured window of the store's own bills; only a bill within that window, and already cached, qualifies for an offline exchange. Its initial default length is 30 days, and it is configurable per tenant (Section 15.2, Offline exchange window).

**R-POS-002 — Full-day offline operation.** A supported till completes the approved trading-day workload offline without losing or duplicating finalised bills. Unknown identities, stock absent from its accepted eligible snapshot, uncached-original-bill returns, new loyalty enrolment and offers delivered after disconnection are prohibited by the current baseline, as are refunds, store credit, no-bill returns, gift-voucher issuance or redemption and credit-note redemption, B2B tax-invoice issue where IRN is required, and any action that needs fresh approval. Nothing bypasses a control because the till is offline. Changing these restrictions requires an approved decision.

**R-POS-003 — Shared balances and independent tills.** Gift-voucher use, store-credit use and loyalty redemption require live shared-balance authority. Other centrally governed tenders such as dues need approved offline limits. The initial supported configuration is one registered till per store: the till is registered online and owns the store's bill series (R-POS-005). Independent multi-till operation per store — its store-LAN stock synchronization, which is not an assumed feature, and its oversell, return-concurrency and FIFO allocation policy — remains a separately approved capability and an explicit prerequisite, not an implementation default; it is not built against the one-till configuration.

**One-till offline defaults.** With one till per store, the cross-till offline oversell case of OQ-30 does not arise. The till's offline authority lasts 24 hours after its last online renewal; after expiry the till cannot finalise new bills until it renews online, and expiry never deletes, hides or releases anything already recorded. No cost, margin or receipt-origin value resides on the till (R-POS-001). A replacement device receives a fresh bill series and never continues the old one (R-POS-005). These defaults answer OQ-31, OQ-32 and OQ-35 for the one-till configuration; a store with more than one active till remains governed by the live-authority and concurrency requirements of this rule, which these defaults do not relax.

**R-POS-004 — Durable local finalisation.** Bill contents, command identity, device sequence progression and pending-sync evidence must commit durably as one local business result before success is shown or receipt issued. Interrupted/failed storage leaves a clear unfinished outcome. Browser restart, duplicate tabs, quota exhaustion, app update and clearing/loss of device storage need approved handling; guarantees must disclose physical-device-loss limits. **Status: Proposed foundation safeguard.**

**R-POS-005 — Device-owned numbering.** Bill display series is `{StoreCode}-{CounterID}-{SequentialNumber}`. Each active till instance has a unique authorised series owner; resets, cloning, replacement, restoration and counter-ID reuse must not recreate prior bill identities. Holes are reported at store open and reconciled without reuse or historic renumbering. Ownership uniqueness must be enforced, not assumed. Replacing a store's registered till device requires online reconciliation of the old device's queue before the new device is issued a fresh bill series; a cloned or restored device may never continue the old series.

**R-POS-006 — Billing journey.** Start authorised till session; resolve alias/context; show ticket and eligibility; calculate applicable price, offer and tax; apply only an authorised manual discount where configuration permits one (none by default, R-PRC-003); record salesperson distinct from login; collect permitted optional customer details; accept supported tenders; finalise immutable bill; print receipt. Optional customer capture cannot block a valid bill. The bill total rounds off to the nearest whole currency unit. Where a business customer's tax registration is captured, it switches the bill between same-registration and inter-registration tax treatment; a tax result that looks wrong is flagged, not blocked unless a specific legal or approved policy restriction applies. An offline or otherwise prohibited action is refused before finalisation while an otherwise valid B2C sale may continue with permitted tender. Where e-invoicing applies, a B2B sale is online only and remains pending until its IRN is captured; no tax invoice prints and no goods leave before issue. When an offer adds a gift SKU, the till prompts the cashier to scan it. Undo is available only before the bill is printed; after that the bill can only be reprinted, never edited.

**R-POS-007 — Tender and receipt outcomes.** Configured tenders include cash, card, UPI, loyalty, dues and gift voucher subject to approved limits. Manual tender recording and provider-confirmed payment remain distinct. Split tenders must reconcile to bill amount. SMS/email and printer failure never creates a second bill or deletes one; retry/reprint preserves original identity and visible delivery outcome. A bill's payment starts unallocated: the cashier chooses all cash, all UPI or all card, or enters an explicit split. Allocated tenders must equal the amount due exactly; under- and over-allocation both block completion. Cash received is recorded separately from the cash tender: blank means exact (the customer gave exactly the cash tender); an explicit zero differs from blank and means no cash was received; an entered amount lower than the cash tender blocks completion as short. Change is calculated only against the cash portion; a cash-received entry when no cash is allocated is rejected, not silently ignored. Invalid tender input never silently keeps an earlier valid amount: every cart or offer change revalidates the split, and a split that no longer matches the amount due is shown as unallocated again. **See OQ-01/OQ-13.**

**R-POS-008 — Durable synchronization.** Connected sync is continuous with periodic/reconnect retry and visible manual retry. Pending, acknowledged, conflicted and failed commands are distinguishable. Stable business idempotency survives restarts and application transport retries. Same identity/different payload is refused. Unsynced bills are never deleted to clear a queue. Old software with queued bills needs a supported compatibility path. **Status: Proposed foundation safeguard.**

**R-POS-009 — Post-sync reconciliation.** Preserve bill identity, original amounts, applied rules and original time/evidence. Compare against the rules applicable to the transaction, not simply sync-time live rules; the till records which working-set version it sold against, and every bill it finalises records that version. Price/stock discrepancies are owned exceptions; no silent repricing. Negative-stock handling follows approved OQ-30 without authorising invented layers, zero costs or sale from unaccepted stock.

**R-POS-010 — Returns and exchanges.** Returns and exchanges follow the store customer-return policy under BP-08 (legal-entity default, with an explicit store override where one exists). With original bill, refund/credit follows paid value subject to approved return limits and prior returns; it is never today's MRP. The outcomes are:

- **Refund:** the store accepts the goods back and returns the eligible amount to the customer. For a bill-backed return the eligible amount is what the customer paid for the returned quantity, subject to the configured policy and prior returns. The permitted refund method follows the applicable tender and finance rules; it creates no new cash-refund entitlement.
- **Exchange:** the store accepts the goods back and the customer chooses replacement goods at their current applicable selling price and offers. If the replacement value equals the accepted return value, no difference is due; if it is higher, the customer pays the difference; if it is lower, the policy configures one of three outcomes — refund the difference, issue the difference as store credit, or disallow the lower-value exchange. An exchange is not restricted to the same SKU unless a configured policy explicitly adds that restriction.
- **Store credit:** the store accepts the goods back and issues the eligible amount as store credit rather than returning money immediately. The policy defines the credit's validity period and any existing redemption controls (R-POS-014).
- **Not accepted:** the store refuses the request when the applicable policy allows none of the requested outcomes or when eligibility conditions are not met. The refused attempt and its reason remain visible where this PRD requires them.



A returned piece is recorded against its specific original bill line, so the same piece can never be returned twice, whether on the same till or, after sync, on another. A returned piece is unavailable to sell again until it has synced and been accepted through the store's own acceptance step; a piece returned as damaged goes to quarantine and follows the damage rules instead. No-bill returns are online-only under the current offline restrictions and need configured policy/manager approval. Record prior returns and authorised quantity/tender restoration. Return quota consumption is tracked only under approved brand-season rules. A return outside the policy's time limit needs a manager override. A return at a store other than the one that issued the bill is a special case for tax and stock, not an ordinary till action. Hygiene items (such as innerwear) are excluded from both return and exchange. Time windows and discount thresholds are policy configuration; for example, a chain default of about 30 days for return and about 45 days for exchange, and a deep-discount policy for goods at or above 50% off that allows no return and only a short exchange window.

**Early limited configuration:** exchange only, against the original bill, online and offline; no refund and no store credit while the later controls remain open. The replacement value must equal or exceed the eligible original paid value of the returned pieces; the customer pays any difference, and a lower-value exchange is refused. The exchange reads the applicable legal-entity default and any approved store override for eligibility and time window. The completed store milestone requires active online store-credit issuance and redemption after the SO-09 policy and applicable OQ-45 controls close; cash refunds and no-bill returns are not thereby approved. **See OQ-44/OQ-45.**

**R-POS-011 — Shift and cash.** Capture opening float, X-report, Z-report and expected-versus-actual cash with variance. One shift is one person, one counter and one drawer. At shift close the cashier declares cash blind, before the system shows expected cash calculated over the named custody boundary and cutoff under R-FIN-015; any variance needs a reason. Produce a daily cash summary by tender mode. At the end of the trading day the store locks the date, expires or keeps held bills, and lists holes in bill numbering, uncosted sales and open exceptions. Record store bank deposits, head-office transfers and petty cash. Shift close distinguishes local finality, unsynced bills and centrally reconciled completion; it must not falsely declare settlement complete while evidence is pending.

**R-POS-012 — Parked carts.** Permit parking and recall before bill finalisation. Held carts crossing the relevant trading-date boundary reprice using applicable available rules. A finalised bill never becomes an editable cart; pending sync does not make it a draft.

**R-POS-013 — Fiscal evidence.** Freeze transaction-specific classification, jurisdiction, rate/rule version, threshold/value basis and tax result. Do not treat receipt PT tax output as the permanent sale rate. Where e-invoicing applies, B2B issue and goods release require captured IRN; a manual-portal flow holds the sale pending until Accounts records the IRN and acknowledgement, while a connected flow obtains it before issue. Credit notes against applicable B2B invoices use online IRN handling. Legal applicability, timing and CA tax baselines must be validated before activation rather than assumed for every invoice.

**R-POS-014 — Customer instruments and store services.** Support customer master, loyalty, dues, gift vouchers, store credit, digital receipts, alteration jobs and home-delivery orders as selected. Keep balances/status changes auditable rather than editable totals. Store credit issued as a customer-return outcome follows R-POS-010 and the return policy's validity period and redemption controls. The completed store milestone includes active online store credit. Before activation, define issuance eligibility, lifecycle, expiry, identity, partial redemption, store scope, permissions, offline restrictions, recovery, reconciliation and acceptance under SO-09 and applicable OQ-45 controls. Customers identified as under 18 receive no marketing during this milestone; any later parental-consent campaign path requires a separate policy decision. **Status: Included, decision-gated.**

**Delivery boundary: Decision-gated.** Configuration selects the POS and store-service capabilities that are activated, and each activated capability resolves its offline authority and device policies. Durable finalisation and duplicate safety remain mandatory.

**R-AN-003 — Reporting isolation and freshness.** Heavy analytics, AI, broad exports and natural-language analysis use approved reporting data, not unbounded operational reads. Natural-language access cannot obtain transactional credentials. Show last applied source position/as-of time, lag, failures and completeness; the binding architecture is in Sections 12.1 and 16.2–16.3. Bounded operational queues/summary views remain distinct from analytical workloads.

**R-AN-004 — Reproducibility and trust.** Reproduce originally published past-period results using their rules and evidence/as-of snapshot. A later restated view is separately labelled. Each dashboard shows approved data-trust/coverage measures; forecast errors and confidence are measured, estimates labelled and missing cost/footfall/funding inputs are not fabricated.

**R-AN-005 — Capture at source.** Any capability that implements a transaction must capture the applicable C-01–C-17 facts in Section 11.3 even when it does not activate the analysis that uses them. Capture requirements do not authorise speculative finance or commercial postings. Unsupported source facts remain missing with known impact.

**R-AN-006 — Metric and surface scope.** Support the required metric groups and S-1–S-9 analytical surfaces described in Section 11. Formula/ID mapping, final screen selection, audience, actions, freshness, permissions and acceptance must be approved before a surface is delivered. Candidate screens are not a final login design. **See OQ-17/OQ-43.**

**R-AN-007 — Human-gated intelligence.** AI proposes; authorised humans decide; deterministic operational logic performs any resulting approved action. Intelligence cannot directly write stock, vendor balances, cash, payroll or books. Draft/suggestion records and human decisions are retained separately with source, model/version, confidence, reviewer, action and measured outcome. **Priority: Critical.**

**R-AN-008 — Numerical authority.** Deterministic code is authoritative for all amounts, quantities, percentages, dates and metric calculations. AI may extract candidate source values with provenance, but cannot invent or authoritatively derive a financial result. A source-extracted number must pass validation and required review before operational use. Narrative is based only on permitted computed facts. **Priority: Critical.**

**R-AN-009 — Escalation and portability.** Use the simplest demonstrated method: deterministic code, then statistical/predictive model, then language model, then agentic interaction only where needed. Record accuracy and confidence honestly. Provider/model/region are configurable behind approved interfaces; thresholds, routing and variation are governed data, not hidden hardcoded policy.

**R-AN-010 — Evaluation gates.** Maintain approved golden sets for extraction, mapping and metrics; no relevant golden set means no release of the assistive capability. Optional AI failure never removes the complete manual workflow. **Priority: Critical. See OQ-20/OQ-21/OQ-43.**

**R-AN-011 — Approved edge catalogue.** E-01–E-26 in Section 11.5 define included candidate assistive outcomes and boundaries. Forecasting uses statistical/ML methods, not an LLM as the numerical forecaster. Free-form transactional querying, autonomous posting and training on tenant data without approved authority are not allowed.

**R-AN-012 — Capability classification.** Treat document reading and mapping as AI-assisted extraction, demand and replenishment as prediction models, dead-stock status as a rules-based derived result, anomaly outputs as indicators, and the daily digest as scheduled assistance over approved computed facts. These are product capabilities, not independent operational agents. Only an explicitly approved analytical assistant may use agentic interaction, and it retains no authority to post transactions. **Status: Included, decision-gated.**

**R-AN-013 — Daily management digest.** Generate a scheduled, role-scoped digest of approved sales, stock, attendance, profitability, return deadlines and owned exceptions. Narrative may summarise only authorised code-computed facts and must show its as-of time and missing/stale inputs. Delivery failure preserves the in-app digest; the digest cannot approve or execute an operational action. **Status: Included, decision-gated.**

**Delivery boundary: Decision-gated.** Before an analytical capability is implemented, OQ-16/OQ-17/OQ-43 and its applicable Section 15 inputs must record approved metrics, data-quality gates, evaluation, users, actions, freshness and acceptance criteria in this PRD.

### 7.13 Shared controls and operating safeguards

**Default metadata:** Owner Product owner / relevant control steward; priority Critical; status Foundation. Applies to all processes.

**R-CTL-001 — Immutable business history.** Preserve append-only business events and official evidence with actor, document/entity, actual/recorded time and appropriate before/after information. Drafts and current projections may change under controlled rules; source history must not be treated as an editable projection. Retention-authorised disposal is separate from operational deletion.

**R-CTL-002 — Central numbering.** Non-POS official documents use centrally controlled gap-free issued series with unique scope, atomic issue and durable replay. Draft references do not consume official PT numbers. Reversals/cancellations retain issued numbers; unexplained holes are visible. Define tenant/entity/type/period series scope, reset and restore rules before activation; do not assume a database sequence guarantees gap-free issue. **See OQ-40.**

**R-CTL-003 — Scope and authority.** Enforce fail-closed tenant/site/brand/action/field scope for reads, writes, search, subscriptions, imports, exports, attachments and background duties. Client-supplied scope is not trusted without verification. Configuration cannot bypass protected PT approval, FIFO, acceptance or evidence rules. One permission system governs authority: versioned role and workflow policy, effective-dated site/brand scope on each role assignment, and the applicable operational step rules. There is no second grant system or lasting bridge.

**R-CTL-004 — Separation of duties.** Record configured thresholds, exact approved revision and distinct human maker/checker identities where required. Shared credentials or service identities cannot impersonate a checker. Privileged scope changes and sensitive payee changes retain approval/audit evidence. Whatever PT approval configuration is chosen (Section 15.2), the preparer of a PT never approves that same PT.

**R-CTL-005 — Exceptions and reason codes.** Every exception has typed kind, owner, SLA, age/overdue state, reason and viable closure route. Closing requires note/evidence and authority. “Drainable” means the kind can be generated, found, assigned and resolved through supported paths. Use governed reason taxonomies, with limited explanatory free text where needed. Goods-path exceptions route to owner queues: short against invoice and excess go to the buyer; a PT-versus-GRN quantity or identity mismatch at approval goes to the PT approver's queue, and the row stays open until someone accepts the difference or reverses; wrong article and damaged-in-transit are raised by the receiving site and owned by quality control: the affected brand's Brand Manager, or Warehouse where the brand has no Brand Manager (Appendix B.5). An arrival without a booking is a legal path, not automatically an exception: it appears on an unbooked-inward watch-list so a booking can be attached later, and becomes an exception only if brand terms are unknown or the commercial model cannot be snapshotted. Each person works from one problems screen whose rows are scoped on the server to what that person may close; a store-only user does not see vendor short or excess, and a buyer does not see till numbering holes.

**R-CTL-006 — Notifications.** Retain owned, searchable in-app records for GRN/PT-needed, SLA breach, approval request, PT official, transfer progress, discrepancy and missing/resolved e-way evidence. PT official means ready for permitted scanning, not automatically sellable. Routing, acknowledgement, escalation/channel/SLA are configured per event/role. External delivery failure does not remove the record or block the operation absent an approved safety gate. Notifications are in-app only in the initial configuration; WhatsApp or other outbound channels remain open under OQ-18. **See OQ-18.**

**R-CTL-007 — Business command safety.** Stable command identity, payload fingerprint, authorised scope and durable result prevent duplicate effect under repeat submit, timeout, retry, restart and replay. Same identity/different content fails explicitly. Stock/value/numbering/approval/audit effects identified as one business operation commit together; external provider effects retain separate outcomes. **Status: Proposed foundation safeguard.**

**R-CTL-008 — External uncertainty and secrets.** Persist approved external intent and references; distinguish queued, attempted, confirmed, failed and unknown outcomes. Reconcile before repeating an uncertain payment or statutory action. Secrets stay outside source code and ordinary logs, are scope-limited and rotatable without rewriting business history. **Status: Proposed foundation safeguard. See OQ-39.**

**R-CTL-009 — Working calendars and deadlines.** A working-day SLA runs against an approved calendar naming a timezone, working weekdays and holiday exclusions. Its deadline is the same local clock time on the target date, counted forward the required number of working days from the triggering event — not a fixed number of 24-hour days later. Each exception's deadline is frozen when the exception is opened; a later reassignment or calendar change never resets it, and the calendar version that explains the original calculation is retained. A real tenant has no implicit fallback working week: a working-day notification policy cannot take effect without a compatible approved calendar in force at that policy's own effective date, and readiness checks require every effective working-day policy to name a valid calendar before the business goes live. Where no calendar is in force for a working-day policy, the affected exception is still opened, owned and resolvable, with no due date — never overdue, sorted last and clearly labelled; it is not refused, because refusing would block the access, export-failure and similar safety paths the exception mechanism protects. Day-, hour- and minute-unit SLAs remain plain elapsed time.

**Delivery boundary: Foundation.** Each activated workflow must identify and prove the controls it depends on. Implementation may add stronger controls but cannot waive these safeguards silently.

## 8. Shared business rules and configuration



### 8.1 Identity, scope and sources of truth

The internal SKU key is stable and opaque. Aliases resolve within authorised issuer/type/effective context and cannot silently redefine identity. Tenant, legal entity/book, registration, actual site, physical condition, acceptance, commercial status and legal ownership are separate concepts. Master changes update future interpretation only; frozen transactional evidence is authoritative for the original transaction.

The GRN owns physical count at arrival. Approval of the reconciled receipt PT makes it official and owns new receipt valuation and layers. Physical scan acceptance owns the release of eligible quantity. Transfer documents own movement evidence, not a new purchase. The finalised sale owns its immutable price/tax/tender evidence. Reports and current stock projections are derived, not independent sources entitled to rewrite these facts.

### 8.2 Quantity conservation and eligibility

A supported movement must account for every selected quantity across source, reservation, transit, destination, return, damage or explicit discrepancy as applicable. Cost-origin allocations conserve original lineage. No stock can be allocated twice through simultaneous online operations. Reservations cannot use current stock that is held or unaccepted. Partial acceptance releases only the accepted eligible portion.

Independent offline tills can share stale observations but cannot be assumed to share current reservations. Offline oversell treatment and strict receipt-layer/FIFO/cost guarantees remain jointly unresolved. OQ-30 must decide the operational policy, permissible trade-offs and evidence before independent multi-till offline selling is activated. Neither “allow negative stock” nor “reconcile later” is a complete costing policy.

For the single-till-per-store configuration, going offline protects a till's assigned selling quantities and its assigned return quantities; they stay protected until that till reconciles online and the protection is explicitly released. Expiry of a till's offline authority never automatically releases a quantity that may already have been consumed offline; a newer working-set snapshot supersedes an older protection rather than releasing it. Release itself is an authorised action with a written reason, and is refused while any of that till's numbered bills has not yet arrived centrally. This does not answer the independent multi-till oversell case, which remains OQ-30.

### 8.3 Document states and correction

Drafts are editable through governed revision history. Official evidence is immutable. A current status may be projected from later events but the original approved payload remains retrievable. Reversal, cancellation, correction, unmatching, adjustment and reissue are distinct authorised actions with references and reasons; they do not delete a past event. Reversal safety includes downstream sale, transfer, reservation, return, payment/export and closed-period effects wherever relevant.

All PT purposes use Draft → Submitted → Approved/Official → Reversed. Submission presents the purpose-specific reconciliation; approval revalidates it against current authority, coverage and source availability. Unexplained differences or invalid submitted content prevent officialisation; authorised receipt discrepancy dispositions preserve truthful unequal source totals. Successful approval atomically issues the official number/version and the receipt, opening or reservation effects defined in R-PT-006. Each supplemental PT receives its own number; reissue retains its original document number with the next official version and renewed approval. A safe pre-dispatch transfer reversal releases its outstanding reservation; after dispatch, actual transit/received quantities require supported movement/counter-document resolution. Issued numbers are not reused. POS and non-POS series both withstand retries and recovery without duplicate identity.

### 8.4 Exact calculations and rule versions

Define currency, permitted unit, quantity precision, value precision, rounding stage/method, allocation remainder rule and numeric range for each supported profile. Money and quantities must use exact decimal/scaled arithmetic appropriate to those definitions; silent binary-floating rounding drift or overflow is unacceptable. Accountable pieces use integral quantities unless an explicitly approved merchandise/unit profile permits otherwise.

Calculation inputs and rule versions are frozen with each official document. Receipt cost, carrying value, ticket price, sale price, taxable transfer value and settlement value must not be conflated. Receipt-approval zero-value restrictions do not prohibit a separately approved free promotional sale reward; such a reward still needs valid receipt cost and applicable tax/funding treatment.

A later rate, price, vocabulary or formula change is a new effective version. It cannot alter historical PT or finalised bills. Backdated configuration requires approval and impact analysis; originally issued results remain retrievable even when a separately authorised restatement is produced.

### 8.5 Tax and jurisdiction configuration

Maintain approved jurisdiction/classification, effective dates, thresholds, value basis, exemptions, rounding and version evidence. No apparel tax ladder is hardcoded in this PRD. Each enabled sale/transfer/finance process freezes the applicable dated result. Receipt PT tax data is neither a permanent sale rate nor proof of input-tax eligibility. Actual legal applicability and timing require qualified tenant approval; the product does not confer legal compliance merely by recording an event.

For e-way evidence, company policy may check all transfers without blocking the recording of actual physical facts. That recording rule is distinct from any legal requirement governing whether the business should undertake a movement. Missing-at-dispatch, later-attached and verified-valid remain separate evidence states.

### 8.6 Approval and policy configuration

Configure role/action/site/brand/field authority, value thresholds, maker/checker separation, supported gate count, reason codes, manual-discount limits, notification/escalation policies, retention, cash/return policies and permitted tenders. Record who approved the exact policy revision and when it became effective. Missing mandatory configuration fails closed rather than choosing a permissive default.

PT uses one designated approval gate by default, with purpose-specific reconciliation and effects. Receipt-discrepancy authority follows R-REC-005; a disposition does not waive identity, valuation or coverage checks. Manual discount limits require configured authority and approved pricing rules. The till offers no manual counter discount (Section 7.7); the manual-discount capability stays configurable and is off in the initial configuration (Section 15.2). Canonical validation, immediate purpose-specific reconciliation, immutable evidence, receipt origin, quantity conservation and site acceptance are not configurable away. Commercial policy and final role/screen details remain decision-gated.

An approved brand/vendor return notice is effective-dated governed configuration sourced from the retained communication. Eligibility, alerts and return lists are derived from that notice and current stock; users do not edit every receipt layer to assign the window.

### 8.7 Time, finality and idempotency

Preserve actual event time, recorded/server time, device time where relevant, business/trading date, time zone and rule-effective time. Clock uncertainty and late arrival of evidence must be visible. A device clock alone must not authorise a backdated offer, tax treatment or expired approval without the approved policy. Server-side checks, background jobs and notification timing use the database server's time, not an application host's or device's clock.

Local bill finality, central ingestion, stock reconciliation, bank payment and statutory acknowledgement are distinct states. Repeating the same command returns its original outcome; using the same identity for changed content fails. Duplicate detection must survive the maximum supported offline/replay window and migration/restore boundaries. Public success responses must identify what has actually committed rather than imply downstream completion.

### 8.8 Integration outcomes and ordinary failure

Business completion and external side effects are separate. Persist required external intent alongside the relevant approved operation, then track provider attempt and confirmation. A timeout is not proof of failure or success. Unknown payment/filing outcomes require reconciliation before another potentially duplicate instruction. Optional AI, email, SMS or analytical service failure cannot stop an otherwise valid operation.

### 8.9 Configuration lifecycle

Supported configuration progresses through draft, validation, approval, scheduled/effective activation and supersession/withdrawal as appropriate. Retain before/after, actor, reason, scope, affected references and compatibility consequences. Superseding a profile must not strand an older till's queued bill or reinterpret its historical inputs. Unsupported arbitrary formulas remain outside the baseline even if a user can upload a spreadsheet containing them.

**Delivery boundary: Decision-gated.** Each activated capability identifies the shared rules and configurations used by its scope and provides approved defaults, owners and acceptance evidence.

### 9.2 Relationship invariants

A tenant has its own operational deployment and multiple possible entities and sites; a legal entity has multiple possible registrations. A booking has many possible GRNs. Each GRN has at most one primary receipt PT plus supplements; each PT identity has at most one live official version and no physical quantity may have duplicate live coverage. Receipt approval reconciles invoice/count evidence, authorised dispositions and existing/proposed coverage before atomically opening receipt origins. Opening uses the approved manifest; transfer reserves existing origins without creating new receipt value. Transfers may draw from several origins and branch across destinations without changing original receipt content. A pending product-master proposal raised from a PT draft may be reused only by a later revision of that same originating PT draft; another document cannot reuse it. A finalised bill line retains applied pricing/tax evidence and the approved origin-allocation outcome; OQ-30 governs offline concurrency rather than changing that relationship implicitly.

A return notice may apply to many eligible stock origins without being copied into or manually frozen on each receipt. A billed-retained custody record references a finalised bill line and removes that quantity from ATS while the physical goods remain at the holding site. A payroll approval may issue one or more payslips; corrections preserve and counter or supersede earlier issued evidence.

An official document may have multiple evidence attachments and later counter documents. Evidence used by several records must remain available while any applicable hold/retention requires it. An immutable bank line can have separately versioned coding and matching actions, but its original facts cannot be overwritten. A recommendation can link to a human decision and later operational action; it is not that action.

## 10. Access, controls, and audit requirements



### 10.1 Authentication and fail-closed authorization

Derive permissions from authenticated human/service identity and authorised assignments. Verify requested tenant, entity, site, brand, action and field against those assignments on every relevant path. Missing/invalid scope authorises nothing and discloses nothing, including counts, search snippets, exports, attachments and live updates. Authentication alone is not authorization. Where a user's authority comes from more than one role or site-and-brand scope assignment, each assignment is a complete entitlement on its own: sites from one assignment and brands, actions or protected fields from another are never recombined into a wider cross-product. Every affected site-and-brand cell in a multi-scope operation must be covered by a qualifying assignment. Named security-review evidence must cover cross-tenant and cross-site denial, not only successful logins.

Ordinary operators use first-party interfaces and permitted APIs. Administrative tools are restricted to explicit support purposes. A service or scheduled task has its own permitted scope and audit identity and does not inherit an anonymous “system can do anything” exemption. Final permission matrix and login design remain OQ-28.

### 10.2 Separation of duties and privileged operations

Maker/checker actions require distinct authorised human identities where configured. A shared password, same human using two roles, unattended agent or reused approval cannot satisfy the rule. Approval binds exact inputs and policy versions. Material revision invalidates the affected approval.

Privileged account, bank-detail, permission, export and configuration changes are visible and reviewable. Production emergency/support access is explicitly authorised, limited, audited and reviewed. Development automation does not routinely receive production secrets or unmasked personal/customer data. The product owner must nominate the responsible reviewer; no additional engineering staffing model is implied.

Step-up (password re-confirmation) is required to retire a staff record, to change logins, role assignments, role or workflow policy, and to act on a privileged-change follow-up review — but not merely to create or edit a staff record. An authority change invalidates affected sessions when it takes effect and receives a distinct-person acknowledgement within one working day (§4.2). Creating a login for an existing person requires an authorised administrator to explicitly confirm that person's identity and their ownership of the login email, with actor and time retained; no identity-document upload or automated email-verification flow is required. The administrator sets and directly shares a temporary password; the user must replace it before doing any business work, and a restricted (must-change-password) session can do nothing except change its own password and manage its own session. Forgotten passwords use the same assisted reset, and any password reset or change ends existing sessions. Passwords are never retained in business audit evidence.

### 10.3 Immutable evidence and privileged bypass

Official evidence cannot be updated or deleted through ordinary operational paths. This is a product requirement, not merely the absence of an edit button. Design must inventory application, bulk, dashboard, direct administrative, import/migration, deployment and restore paths; specify who can access each; and distinguish prevention, tamper detection and recovery.

Official tables are protected by revoked UPDATE and DELETE grants and raising triggers for every application, bulk, import, migration and restore role. Superuser and provider-administrator paths are detected, not prevented: a per-row hash chain, chain heads anchored to write-once storage every 5 minutes, an off-box verifier under a separate account, and continuously streamed pgaudit logs (Section 16.6). Host root can defeat on-box controls; the undetected window is up to 5 minutes on unanchored rows, accepted by the product owner. OQ-33 remains open until the Section 16.10 proof demonstrates detection, stopped-anchor and stopped-log alerts.

Hashes help identify changed content only when the trusted originals/manifests and authority boundaries are also protected. Do not claim that storing a hash beside mutable content makes evidence unalterable. Rebuildable projections may be updated, but never substituted for protected source evidence. Retention-authorised disposal is a separate controlled action, not an operational edit route.

### 10.4 Sensitive data and offline devices

Protect cost, margin, bank, payroll, payslip, employee identity and personal customer fields according to approved purpose and scope. Do not send unauthorised sensitive fields to a browser and then rely on hidden UI elements. Local/offline computation requiring cost or margin inputs creates a product-security decision: OQ-31 must determine what may reside on a till, device assumptions and permitted offline functions. For the single-till-per-store configuration the recorded decision is that no cost, margin or receipt-origin value resides on the till; OQ-31 remains open for any wider till configuration.

Booking cost is hidden from the Store Person and Warehouse roles by default; only roles granted cost/margin scope see it. Requesting stock value at cost without the required cost-field authority is explicitly refused (a field-denied outcome), not silently answered with cost omitted; MRP-basis value needs no special field authority. A brand-scoped stock reader may see unidentified goods at an otherwise authorised site, because such goods carry no brand yet to scope them out of view; this discloses no other brand's data.

The initial protected-field defaults are: Owner may read financial, employee-private and customer records within scope; Accounts may read financial and employee-private data plus customer identity needed for billing; Brand Manager may read cost and margin for assigned brands and anonymised customer reporting; Warehouse may read and enter cost only on PTs they prepared, never general valuation or margin; Store Person may read operational and service-needed customer details at assigned sites; Admin may administer accounts but has no Money, business financial, employee-private or customer-profile access. Operational staff identity is distinct from private employee fields. Field authority governs reads and writes across APIs, search, exports, files, events and till datasets; a prohibited write is rejected. These defaults are configurable only through the versioned policy and fixed safeguards in §4.2–4.3.

Define local-authority lifetime, clock uncertainty, revocation behaviour, session expiry and reconnect handling. Instant revocation cannot be represented as effective at an already disconnected till without an actual communication path. OQ-32 must approve the offline lease/expiry policy and what happens to already finalised unsynced work; that work cannot simply be erased. For the single-till-per-store configuration the recorded decision is that a till's offline authority lasts 24 hours after its last online renewal; after expiry the till cannot finalise new bills until it renews online, and expiry never deletes, hides or releases anything already recorded. OQ-32 remains open for any wider till configuration.

### 10.5 Evidence handling and privacy

Preserve original bytes/hash, source, preparation/conversion history, approved versions, actor/time, reason and linked document/origin. Uploaded files, spreadsheets, images, extracted text and external messages are untrusted data, not instructions to an agent or authority to post. Validate supported formats/sizes and safely handle malicious content, formula injection and accidental sensitive-data disclosure.

Use encryption in transit and at rest, scoped file retrieval, managed secrets and logged access/export where required. Collect personal data only for approved purposes. Define retention, legal hold, correction and permitted deletion without removing immutable evidence that must be retained. Retention duration, residency, backup locations and third-party data use need tenant/legal approval rather than provider defaults.

### 10.6 Audit and control evidence

Audit records identify actor/service, tenant/site scope, action, actual and recorded times, document/version, relevant before/after, authority and reason. Record refusal, reversal, late evidence, support access and compliance resolution where needed without leaking secrets. Draft correction history and official correction history remain distinct.

Each critical guarantee must have a control record identifying requirement, threat/failure path, selected enforcement, limitation/bypass, test method, observed result and reviewer. A database constraint, command, UI check and operational policy are different mechanisms and must be described honestly. Section 14 defines the minimum acceptance scenarios and Section 16.10 defines the mandatory technical proofs.

Site and governed organisation-master screens show real, scoped, newest-first paginated history to the roles already entitled to review those records; an empty or absent history view is not acceptable delivered behaviour. Each history entry names its action in plain words, never an internal code. The actor is shown by name only where the reader's own grants already let them see that person; otherwise the entry reads “Restricted person”.

**Delivery boundary: Foundation.** Every activated capability must define and test the access, privacy, evidence and audit controls required by its selected workflows; these controls cannot be silently deferred where the workflow depends on them.

### 14.2 Migration, opening and coexistence

Define the cutover boundary for each source and process, identify which system may write at each point, and preserve in-progress bookings, receipts, stock holds/transit, bills, returns, cash and approved finance obligations. Avoid overlapping writers that can create duplicate business events. Mixed-period or read-only legacy history must be labelled as such.

Opening stock is a governed operational manifest, not an invented purchase or accounting opening journal. Preserve source file/batch/row, original identifiers, supplied valuation, physical condition and known lineage. Unknown older origins remain honestly unknown, not fabricated receipts. Reject invalid submitted rows or duplicates and retain reconciliation by site, SKU/unit, condition and value basis. Apply R-INV-011 for physical verification, authorised opening variances and explicit exclusions; preserve the original manifest and all unresolved rows. Accepted stock status requires evidence; cutover does not release a hold by default.

Financial opening balances, backfill, historic dates and statutory-book coexistence require the separate approved finance transition policy. Each tenant/book defines its own approved start date. Reconcile operational openings to their manifest and external books separately; neither comparison is a new commercial gate that changes the settled operational opening contract.

### 14.3 Restore, hosting change and product exit

A tested export/recovery bundle must include consistent business data and relationships, original files/manifests, configuration and calculation versions, audit/corrections, command identities, issued series ownership, scope metadata and relevant pending external outcomes. Define snapshot/source positions and how later events are captured without a gap or duplicate. Verify row/file completeness and independent quantity/value/bill/control totals.

The stack has no platform vendor: database, backend, auth, jobs and realtime move together as plain PostgreSQL and the approved Django/Python backend (Section 16.2). The product requires preserved business meaning and a demonstrable export path; the export bundle and the independent reader are that path. A future hosting move must separately resolve secrets, DNS, email provider, evidence-store copy, old tills and the source-of-truth switchover. [R3][R4]

Restoration must not duplicate external payments, reuse a bill number, lose a retained file or reinterpret a queued old-version bill. Reopening writes needs approved reconciliation and owner acceptance. Operational runbooks, migration execution tasks and rollback steps remain linked supporting documents, not a project schedule in this PRD.

### 14.5 Acceptance and release authority

Overall business acceptance requires approved scope, resolved blockers, passed applicable integrity/security/device/recovery scenarios, reconciled transition data, nominated operating owners and visible support/monitoring routes. The business owner signs against actual evidence, not a generated checklist marked complete without tests.

A known exception needs its impact, owner, permitted scope, compensating controls and explicit approval. It cannot silently override an invariant; a genuine weakening requires an approved requirements change. A capability appears in a release claim only after it is built and its applicable evidence passes. Complete-product acceptance means every included requirement is delivered and the integrated specification passes; scheduling it in a plan is never enough.

**Delivery boundary: Decision-gated.** Configuration and the applicable Section 15 decisions select the tenant inputs and acceptance scenarios that apply to each activated capability, and its detailed expected results and evidence are supplied before release.

### 15.2.1 Binding product rules — tenancy, receiving, quarantine and money

The following product rules are binding and take precedence over any conflicting draft, review note, technical design or current implementation. A technical design may refine schemas, interfaces, error outcomes and delivery order; it cannot weaken these rules.

1. **Tenant-owned inherited masters.** Every role, vendor, brand, legal entity, registration and site belongs to exactly one tenant. Tenant ownership is mandatory and enforced below application code. Masters migrated from a tenant's earlier systems gain tenant ownership without changing their identity or history. Codes are unique within a tenant for roles, vendors, brands, legal entities and sites; names are display labels and need not be unique. A registration number is unique within a tenant, and a nonblank legal-entity PAN is unique within a tenant. A nonblank vendor GSTIN identifies at most one vendor within a tenant. A registration must reference a legal entity in the same tenant, and effective registrations for the same number may not overlap: while registrations carry no effective period, one record per registration number within the tenant satisfies this, and once they carry effective periods, overlapping periods for the same number are refused. A site must reference a registration and a legal entity in the same tenant. The same code or registration may exist in a different tenant. Every relationship among these records, including role grants and vendor-brand, entity-registration and registration-site links, must be same-tenant. No null tenant, tenant-blind (global) uniqueness rule or application filter substitutes for these constraints.
2. **Ordinary receipt PT eligibility.** An ordinary primary or supplemental receipt PT may cover only a live, uncovered portion from its GRN whose source condition is `good`, whose identity and profile-defining attributes are resolved, and which has no unresolved receipt-disposition hold. Coverage also includes approved counter-GRN increase lots, taken after the original GRN's own lots. A separately approved `accept_excess` decision may make the named good excess portion eligible for a supplement. Wrong or unidentified goods that the business explicitly accepts may likewise go on a normal receipt PT as a supplement once their identity is resolved and the required evidence is available; the original discrepancy stays on record, the acceptance does not erase it, and any concurrent damage hold stays in force. Damaged goods are never converted this way; they follow their own quarantine and value-damage route. No other discrepancy decision converts damaged, wrong or unidentified goods into ordinary receipt PT eligibility. A PT line must use the eligible lot's resolved SKU; relabelling cannot substitute another SKU or erase source condition.
3. **Held disputed goods.** Damaged, wrong and unidentified quantities remain in quarantine. Unresolved excess remains in excess hold. They stay visible, unvalued unless a permitted valuation decision is completed, unavailable to sell or to transfer as ordinary stock, and removable only through their approved return, disposal, identity-resolution, excess-acceptance or value-damage route, or through rejection of a mistaken damage report under rule 10. Approving clean quantities neither covers nor releases them. Quarantined goods, including quarantined goods not yet covered by an official PT, may nevertheless move between sites while held: store quarantine to warehouse quarantine by an approved internal transfer, and store or warehouse quarantine to a vendor or brand by RTV. Such a move uses only the GRN/custody information actually available; it never invents identity, cost, tax or stock-layer detail, never makes the goods sellable or allocatable as ordinary stock, and does not release or alter their hold, valuation state or eligibility; destination receipt retains their quarantine condition. Damage may be recorded during GRN creation, after GRN creation but before PT creation, or after PT creation; after PT creation the normal damage-reporting flow applies, and before PT only the information actually available from the GRN and later evidence is retained. Confirmed damaged goods may remain in store or warehouse quarantine indefinitely; movement is optional.
4. **Value damage.** `value_damage` means the business elects to establish operational memo value for an already-counted damaged portion that it keeps. Physical quantity and source condition do not change. The portion remains in quarantine under its damage hold and ATS and transferable quantity remain zero. The decision identifies the exact GRN, custody lot/range and quantity; retains condition/damage evidence, resolved identity, source value/tax evidence, reason and maker; and freezes the evidenced positive ticket MRP. Unit operational value is that MRP—not a derived purchase cost, zero, a current master value or an accounting value—and the total is quantity × MRP in integer paise. It creates no purchase, payable, tax-credit or general-ledger effect. It requires the effective `value_damage` approval policy, a different human approver, matching tenant/site/brand scope and satisfied quantity/value thresholds. Until this complete process is built and explicitly enabled, a `value_damage` request must be refused as unsupported and must leave the existing unvalued damage hold unchanged; success without the value event is prohibited.
5. **Effective profile and tax versions.** Every receipt/opening PT draft explicitly selects one approved profile version for the same tenant, purpose and applicable site/brand scope. The selected version and every referenced vocabulary, formula and rate version are pinned to the draft revision and frozen on the official version. Tax selection additionally matches tenant, registration/jurisdiction, classification, currency and purpose. Effective periods are half-open: the start instant is included and the end instant is excluded; a null end means no scheduled end. The applicable instant is the document's recorded business event time: actual arrival for receipt, manifest cutoff for opening, and source official-version time for copied transfer evidence. Exactly one matching version must exist at that instant. Missing, future, expired, overlapping or wrong-scope versions fail closed. Approval rechecks the pinned IDs, their approval state, scope and applicability to that instant; a later superseding version does not replace a valid pinned version or reinterpret an official document. Backdating requires the approved impact review. A PT line's attributes use known profile keys only: at most 100 entries, free text of at most 240 characters each, and exactly one governed value or an explicit "unknown" per entry.
6. **Exact monetary input and output.** INR values cross public JSON boundaries as base-10 integer-paise strings and are stored/calculated with exact decimal or scaled-integer arithmetic, never binary floating point. Spreadsheet monetary cells may contain whole rupees or at most two decimal places; blank means unknown, an explicit `0`/`0.00` means genuine zero, and non-finite, out-of-range or excess-precision input is refused rather than rounded. Derived calculations retain exact intermediate precision and use decimal half-up only at the stage and scale named by the pinned profile. Receipt/opening unit cost and MRP must both be greater than zero and cost must not exceed MRP; other domains may permit genuine zero only when their own rule says so. Unknown unit or aggregate value is null/omitted with completeness metadata, never zero. Generated and re-ingested spreadsheets must preserve the exact integer-paise value, including odd-paise, half-rounding, maximum-value and round-trip cases.
7. **Opening boundary.** Generic opening-manifest, verification, opening-PT and acceptance behaviour is built and proven with synthetic tenant-neutral data. Actual tenant data preparation, mapping, reconciliation and loading are a separately gated migration activity (OQ-29); synthetic proof does not stand in for it, and no setting bypasses that gate. Any non-synthetic tenant's opening approval therefore remains disabled until those migration sources are recorded (OQ-29), and no supplied season bypasses that gate. Migration assigns each opening row its real season from the item's own data (R-MDM-011). An opening row may carry the explicit, deliberately chosen and audited value *unknown historical season* instead of a real season only where no season can be found for that item; it is never inferred, defaulted or derived, and it loosens no other required field (Section 15.2, Opening stock). A supplied mapped season in a synthetic test's approved profile does not change that meaning.
8. **Store-only records.** Staff, locations, goods exceptions and exception notifications that carry no brand belong to their store. Any grant at that store (store, entity, tenant or store-plus-brand scope) covers them there, whatever brand the grant is limited to; a brand-only grant covers none. Goods, documents, stock and money keep the strict per-brand rule. An exception note may therefore mention another brand's document to a store-plus-brand reader; this is accepted. Ruled by Anand on 14 September 2026.
9. **Tax rate scope.** GST rates by HSN are the same in every state, so one tenant-wide tax-rate version may apply at every registration. Jurisdiction-dependent treatment (for example inter-state versus intra-state) belongs to each transaction's own classification, not to the receipt PT's tax-rate selection. Ruled by Anand on 14 September 2026.
10. **Correcting a mistaken damage report.** A different authorised person from the reporter may reject a damage report as mistaken. The rejection requires a reason and is a recorded correction event with actor and time; it preserves the complete history and never silently edits or erases the original report. Rejecting the report removes the damage condition and the damage quarantine only: the goods may return to an eligible stock location only if their identity is resolved and no other hold applies, and any independent hold remains in force.



Configuration scope, effective periods, PT profile selection and approval-policy pinning follow these rules.

**Configuration scope and effective periods.** A configuration scope names tenant, entity, sites, SBUs, brands and an optional list of purposes; an empty dimension means every value of it, and a payload field that repeats a scope dimension (an approval policy's sites, brands or purpose; a rate's brand) must equal the scope. Vocabulary, identity-profile, barcode-range and business-profile configurations apply tenant-wide only; two vocabularies in force for the same dimension fail closed, and a crosswalk may cite only a configuration version currently in force, never a withdrawn profile. Two versions are mutually exclusive when they share a configuration kind, that kind's key (approval: action; profile and identity profile: family; vocabulary: dimension; series: entity, type and financial year; reasons: action; notifications: event; workflow: operation; non-trading: site), scopes that can reach the same place and overlapping effective periods; such an overlap is refused. Barcode ranges are never mutually exclusive. When it is unclear whether two scopes meet, they are treated as meeting. A later version of the same draft, or of the exact same scope, supersedes the earlier one: the earlier effective period ends where the later begins, and a later version of the same draft ends every earlier version of that draft whatever scope it named. A version that ends an earlier one takes effect no earlier than its own approval, unless it is a reviewed backdate (a start before today, with its impact list); a first version keeps its drafted start. A successor carrying an end date may not end a predecessor that would otherwise run longer; that is refused as an overlap. A setup draft may carry an end date. An approved configuration version may be withdrawn for invalidity by one configuration approver whose authority covers the version's whole scope, after a fresh password confirmation; withdrawal stops every later use of that version, including already-pinned drafts and pending approvals. Ending a version early with no successor and no withdrawal is not supported.

**Profiles, rates and tax.** A PT profile names its own rate version and its own tax version. HSN tax rules match by exact instant: rules for one HSN may touch but not overlap, and the slabs within one rule may not overlap; a rule whose start carries no time zone never applies. A receipt PT's profile, rate, tax and vocabulary versions must all apply, at the actual arrival time, to the GRN site, the arrival brand, every line brand and the receipt purpose; pricing at submission and at approval both use that same arrival time.

**Approval policy.** One approval-policy version carries exactly one limit: an inclusive maximum quantity and an inclusive maximum value in paise, either of which is optional; it may also require a password confirmation. Its unknown-value branch is either refuse (the default) or quantity-only; an unknown value matters only when the policy carries a value limit and is never treated as zero. Tiered limits (for example, a higher approver above some amount) are not part of this rule set. With no approval policy in force for its purpose, submitting a receipt PT, a PT reversal, a counter-GRN or a disposition is refused; because every disposition needs a distinct checker, no disposition proceeds without a policy in force for its purpose (Section 15.2, Disposition checker). Configuration approval and product-proposal confirmation keep their own fixed approvers regardless. A policy judges a receipt PT by its pieces and by pieces × the pinned per-piece purchase rate (P RATE); a PT reversal by the same figures taken from the official lines; a counter-GRN by total pieces changed, with value treated as unknown; and a disposition by its pieces, with value treated as unknown. The policy purposes are receipt, counter-GRN and each disposition kind; every disposition is decided by a distinct checker within its policy's limits. Submission pins the approval policy in force at that moment and the decision looks it up again. If a newer version of that policy has come into force, or the policy has been withdrawn, expired or lost scope, or two versions now match, the decision is refused as stale and the maker must submit again; a newer version that starts later than now does not make the pinned policy stale. The approver must hold one of the policy's permitted roles with the relevant action granted over every site and brand the subject touches, or the decision is refused; a rejection likewise needs a permitted role but not a policy still in force. A request made before any policy was pinned to it may be rejected but never approved. Every decision — approved, refused or stale — records the policy version used, its limits, the subject's own scope with its pinned pieces and value, the policy's scope, and only the specific grants that justified the decision.

A decision that has not been recorded in this PRD as an approved rule, default or Section 15.2 entry is not product authority, whatever draft, review note or working file it appears in.

### 16.3 Application and domain boundaries

Build one modular codebase containing the Django backend and React frontend. Feature modules follow the business capabilities in Sections 6–11. Pure pricing, tax selection, rounding, eligibility, FIFO allocation, reconciliation, payroll and allocation calculations remain versioned Python independent of HTTP views and ORM persistence. Where offline POS needs the same calculation in the browser, maintain the corresponding versioned TypeScript implementation independent of React and prove Python/TypeScript parity against the same approved reference cases. Server-side state and permission validation remain authoritative and run inside the committing database transaction; browser calculations do not authorise a server posting.

Every public API route validates parameters, query, body and response at runtime. Output schemas omit unauthorised fields. TypeScript types and hidden UI elements do not provide runtime or field security. Use indexed bounded queries, explicit pagination, measured file/document limits and durable jobs for large exports or reports. Stable business IDs and explicit provider-ID mappings preserve portability.

Realtime signals contain scoped identifiers only. The client performs an authorised refetch and resumes from a durable cursor. Reporting, exports and intelligence are isolated from core operational workload and expose lag, as-of time and completeness.

### 16.4 Atomic business-command contract

Every official state-changing business command runs in one Django `transaction.atomic()` block over PostgreSQL with `SERIALIZABLE` isolation explicitly established before business queries; `atomic()` alone does not establish that isolation level. Use the common command kernel for command identity, locking and whole-transaction retries. Inside that transaction, in this order:

1. set trusted tenant and user session variables;
2. insert or resolve the scoped command identity, content fingerprint and stored outcome;
3. acquire `FOR UPDATE` locks in a documented fixed key order;
4. revalidate current identity assignments, tenant/site/brand/action/field scope, separation of duties, expected state/revision, configuration versions and eligible quantities;
5. write every stock, value, numbering, approval, evidence link, audit, external intent, job and command-outcome record that belongs to the business result; and
6. commit once or leave no partial effect and no consumed official number.

Identical replay returns the stored original result. Different content under the same command identity fails. Retry the complete transaction only for PostgreSQL `40001` and `40P01`, at most five times with jitter. Several requests are not one atomic business action. Maximum supported documents remain one transaction; do not split inward/officialisation to work around assumed database limits.

Application, worker, import, migration and restore code never connects as superuser or with UPDATE/DELETE rights on official tables. Official rows revoke those grants and use raising triggers. Corrections append rows. Projections may be rebuilt from protected evidence but cannot replace or rewrite it.

### 16.5 Offline and device contract

Offline POS is durable product work. Before showing completion or printing, one IndexedDB transaction commits the immutable bill, device/counter sequence progression and pending-sync evidence. Interruption before that commit leaves a recoverable cart and no final bill; interruption after it leaves a restart-safe bill. Unsynced finalised work is never deleted to clear an error.

Keep local finality, server acknowledgement and business reconciliation separate. Every bill retains its device identity, sequence, contract version, source event/recording times, rule/profile versions, prices, taxes, tenders and original contents. Upload is authenticated and idempotent. Duplicate/replayed content returns its original acknowledgement; conflicting content is quarantined. An older supported contract uploads without reinterpretation; an unsupported contract is visibly refused while the bill remains locally retained.

OQ-30/31/32/35/45 must be closed for the configuration being activated before their affected offline behaviour is activated. The approved device/browser/scanner/printer set, local working set, lease and session rules, clone/replacement policy, allocation and return-conflict treatment and compatibility window are recorded directly in Section 15, including the one-till decisions recorded against OQ-30/31/32/35 and the early exchange-only return profile and the later online store-credit gate recorded under OQ-45. Until then, an implementer may build shared storage/sync foundations but cannot invent those policies.

Test storage exhaustion, interrupted finalisation, restart, duplicate tabs, device clone/replacement, upgrades, stale snapshots, clock uncertainty, expired/revoked authority, repeated upload and physical device loss. Local storage and device keys are not described as tamper-proof hardware.

### 16.6 Security, evidence and audit

Authenticate every public path and derive tenant, entity, site, SBU, brand, action and field scope from trusted assignments. Apply the same checks to list, detail, search, mutation, file, import, export, SSE and background duties. Cross-scope access returns not found without counts or snippets. Sensitive cost, margin, bank, payroll, employee and customer fields never reach an unauthorised client payload.

Maker/checker operations bind exact revisions and policy versions and require distinct authorised human identities. A shared account, service identity or one person holding two roles cannot self-check. Privileged actions use the approved step-up mechanism. An approved password-only authentication exception applies only as this product's initial configuration default; it does not silently decide later authentication policy, and extending it beyond that initial default requires an explicit approved change.

Step-up authentication is required for staff retirement and for changes to login, scoped role assignment, role/workflow policy or privileged-review records; ordinary staff creation or edits do not require it unless a configured policy says otherwise. The platform administrator alone cannot create tenant staff or review a tenant's privileged changes; company-owner authority is required for those. The one exception is a new tenant's first company owner: tenant onboarding creates that one Owner login, once, with a temporary password the owner must replace at first sign-in; the step is audited, and afterwards the platform administrator cannot create tenant staff. Staff retirement is a governed action; the company owner holds it in the initial role policy. Retiring a staff member is refused while they own an open exception. Ending their future staff and role assignments closes the retirement path rather than independently blocking it.

Official evidence preserves original bytes/canonical payload, hash, source, actor, actual and recorded times, configuration/rule versions and linked corrections. Object storage enforces write-once retention. Official database rows use grants, raising triggers and a per-row hash chain; chain heads and row counts anchor to separate write-once storage every five minutes. An off-box verifier uses a separate account, and pgaudit/PostgreSQL logs stream off the database host. Missing anchors, stopped logs and mismatches alert their owner. Host-root/provider-admin tampering is detected rather than prevented; the accepted undetected window is at most five minutes on unanchored rows and must pass the Section 16.10 proof before release.

Uploads, spreadsheet cells, email, extracted text and provider responses are untrusted data, never instructions or posting authority. Validate file signature, size and safe name; preserve source bytes/hash; prevent spreadsheet formula injection; do not render untrusted HTML or fetch its remote content. Secrets and real tenant/personal data do not appear in logs, fixtures, payload diagnostics or committed files.

Audit identifies actor/service, tenant/site scope, action, times, document/version, before/after where applicable, authority, reason and outcome. Record refusals, reversals, retries, late evidence, external calls, privileged/support access and configuration change. For each critical guarantee, retain requirement → threat/failure path → mechanism → limitation/bypass → test → observed result → reviewer.

### 16.7 External effects, jobs and failure

## Appendix A. Canonical PT file

This appendix is normative. It defines the meaning of the canonical PT file: the priced, tax-complete record of a movement of stock and the merchandising grid that every piece of stock is filed against. It describes the meaning of the shape, not one workbook copy. The main-text rules it serves are in §7.3 (product identity and canonical profiles) and §7.5 (physical receipt and canonical PT).

### A.1 Purpose and uses

A PT file is also known in the business as the price ticket, article master or barcode master. Vendor files, self-authored lines and inter-site transfers all land on this one shape or are converted onto it.

There are two jobs and one format:

1. **Receipt PT** — what arrived from a vendor, with price and tax.
2. **Transfer PT** — what moved from one site to another.

Both use the same columns. They differ only in origin: an external file, a self-authored file, or a file generated from existing registered stock and its preceding documents (§7.5 R-PT-003). An opening PT uses the same shape with migration context (§7.5 R-PT-003, §7.6).

The column set in A.4 is the currently published shape. It can grow, shrink, be renamed or be split through a new profile version (§7.3 R-MDM-006). The job cannot change: **one row is one sellable identity, and that row is the source of truth for the stock that hangs off it.**

### A.2 Source of truth for stock

- Nothing is sellable until it has a PT row (§7.6 R-INV-002). A scan at the till looks up the barcode this file defines. Reporting, allocation, valuation and tax all read the same row. There is no second master that independently describes the piece (§8.1).
- Every stock quantity hangs off a PT row through its barcode. Stock is a count under that barcode, not one barcode per physical piece: five interchangeable units share one barcode and a quantity of five (§7.3 R-MDM-005).
- From one row the business can recover everything that matters about a unit in hand: what it is (item, design, colour, size, fit, gender and category axes); whose line it is (brand); which season it was filed under; how it is taxed (HSN, input and output slabs); how it is priced and valued (MRP, cost base, purchase or transfer rate, margin); and how many units that identity represents on this document.
- The merchandising axes are how the stock is reported, allocated and offered. The money axes are how it is inwarded and valued.
- Later master edits do not rewrite history. A document stays frozen at what its row said on the day it became official (§7.3 R-MDM-008).

**Barcode rules.**

- Branded goods keep the vendor barcode when it is unique to that identity.
- Untagged goods receive a system-generated barcode, written into the same BARCODE field as vendor barcodes. There is no separate field for a generated code. The branded/untagged test and the tagging step are in §7.5.
- Several interchangeable pieces share one barcode. On-hand stock is the running count against that alias.
- Two different identities sharing one vendor barcode is an exception to be surfaced, never a silent merge (§7.3 R-MDM-002, R-MDM-003).



### A.3 Workbook layout

A canonical PT workbook has two kinds of sheet.


| Kind       | Role                                                                                                                                                                                                                    |

| Work sheet | The document. One row is one sellable identity. A header row is followed by data rows. Totals may sit above the header row so that filtered sums still work.                                                            |


Authoring may be split across several work sheets (by person, site or queue). That split is operational, not a second schema: every work sheet copies the same column set.

Layout details — which column letter is left unused, where the suggested pair sits, freeze panes, filter ranges — are incidental. If the grid is reordered, a column is added, or a derived field is dropped from the export view, the source of truth is still the **resolved line**: identity + merchandising + quantity + money + tax. The grid is an export view of a resolved line, not a requirement that the system store every spreadsheet column forever. ### A.5 Master lists

Each master column is an independent closed list. Typical lists:


| List        | Role               | Rules that survive a format change                                                                      |
| ----------- | ------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Season      | Time code          | Patterned and generatable (for example half-year plus month stamp). A row must pick a real code.        |
| Brand       | Issuer of the line | Duplicates and odd spacing are list-quality problems, not extra columns.                                |
| Colour      | Appearance         | Typos and misfiled grades become aliases or a separate configured axis (A.7), never silent new colours. |
| Gender      | Audience           | Small closed set.                                                                                       |
| Subcategory | Merchandising      | Official values only on the work sheet.                                                                 |
| Type        | Merchandising      | Official values only on the work sheet.                                                                 |
| Item        | Article kind       | Suggested subcategory and type may be looked up from here.                                              |
| Fit         | Cut or set         | A mix of true fits, brand cut names and set compositions is common dirt.                                |
| Size        | Measurement token  | Carries a size kind wherever the system must not confuse waist `32` with age `32` or `32B`.             |
| Tax slab    | Legal rates        | The formula may not emit every slab on the list; overrides exist for that reason.                       |


Suggested subcategory and suggested type sit beside item on the master as a **hint table**. They are not a second copy of the official subcategory and type lists.

**Dirt is allowed in the lists, not in the job.** Dropdowns are strict; the lists behind them are often dirty (typos, mixed meanings, combined suggestion values). The product migrates vocabulary rather than copying dirt as canonical codes. Retired strings stay as aliases so that historical rows still resolve (§7.3 R-MDM-002).

### A.6 Identity

The sellable identity on a work-sheet row is **Brand × Design × Colour × Size**, carried by a **barcode**. In this profile DESIGN plays the Style role of §7.3 R-MDM-004.

The other merchandising fields — season, gender, subcategory, type, item and fit — describe that identity for operations and reporting. They do not create a second identity.

An article code may have no column of its own. On branded rows, DESIGN is often the style code. On generated rows, the barcode is the identity.

### A.7 Colour and substitute tags

Economy, Medium and Premium are examples of tags used when actual colour information is unavailable. They are intentional operational codes, not automatically invalid colours and not a mandatory universal grade.

A business may configure the COLOR field as:

- colour only;
- grade/tag only; or
- both colour and grade/tag.

These options are available through settings and business-format preparation. The product preserves the meaning of a configured tag without inventing an unknown colour. A common tag alone does not prove that two articles are the same identity.

### A.8 Configuration of the file

Settings govern the columns, labels, order, data types and requiredness; permitted vocabularies, defaults and aliases; which values are supplied, selected, inherited, looked up or calculated; formula inputs, dependencies, rates, additions, thresholds, rounding and permitted overrides; categories, subcategories, types, items, size systems, seasons and additional tags; currency (INR for the first tenant); approval permissions, gates and site/brand scope; and which configured fields appear in the in-app table and the Excel export. Formats and calculation rules are versioned, and an individual upload never silently redefines the meaning of historical data (§7.3 R-MDM-006, R-MDM-007; §15.2.1 rule 5).

### A.9 Money, tax and validation

- **Calculation directions.** Base-to-ticket, ticket-to-purchase and both-supplied, with their formulas, rounding and slab selection, are stated in the receipt-costing rule in §7.3. Appendix C holds the approved expected results.
- **Tax.** Tax columns encode jurisdiction rules — item and type exceptions, value thresholds and an override list. Those rules can change without renaming the columns. No one retailer's tax ladder is built into the meaning of INPUT TAX or OUTPUT TAX (§8.5). The applicable slab comes from the pinned tax version (§15.2.1 rules 5 and 9).
- **Frozen cost.** P RATE is frozen on the document when it becomes official. A transfer line carries its origin's frozen cost and MRP unchanged (§7.6 R-INV-004).
- **Required values.** A receipt or opening line needs P RATE greater than zero, MRP greater than zero and P RATE not above MRP (§7.5 R-PT-005; §15.2.1 rule 6).
- **Bounds.** Money values stay within ₹0.00–₹999,999,999.99. Quantity stays within 1–999,999. Configured percentages (transport, margin, GST) stay within 0.00–99.99. A row outside any bound cannot be submitted, and a percentage outside its bound is refused before any division is attempted.
- **Money cells.** Spreadsheet money cells hold whole rupees or at most two decimal places; blank means unknown; an explicit zero means genuine zero; non-finite, out-of-range or excess-precision input is refused, not rounded (§15.2.1 rule 6).
- **Supplied check values.** Where a file carries its own value in a derived column, it is compared with the computed value within a configured tolerance in minor currency units. Within tolerance the row is accepted; outside it the row is blocked for review and the supplied value is kept as evidence. The computed value is never replaced by the uploaded one (§7.3 R-MDM-007).
- **Export.** The in-app table and the `.xlsx` download reproduce the frozen profile and frozen values; a download creates no stock event (§7.5 R-PT-010; §16.8).



### A.10 What must remain true after any format change

1. One PT row, one barcode identity, and a quantity under that identity.
2. That row is sufficient to sell, value, tax and describe the unit.
3. Derived views can be recomputed. Typed identity and ticket facts cannot be invented after the fact.

- **More roles are added by configuration when needed.** A new role is added only when the product needs one, not by default. Access for a role is set and retuned as configuration by an authorised administrator, never by changing code.
- **One permission system.** Authority is the combination of versioned role section levels and protected-field permissions (B.3), effective-dated scoped role assignments, and versioned step rules of the applicable workflow (§4.3, §7.13). There is no second grant system or lasting bridge.
- **Scope is per assignment.** Scope words in the grid such as "Own store" and "Assigned brands" describe the site and brand set on each person's role assignment, not a level. Explicit all-sites/all-brands includes future members; selected sets do not expand automatically, and empty scope grants nothing. One assignment's role and field powers never combine with another assignment's scope.
- **Store Person is one role for the initial pilot.** Managers use personal PINs for governed counter overrides. A Store Manager role is added within this permission system before multi-store rollout, after OQ-28 defines its authority and separation rules; the initial six-role grid does not silently grant the later role access.

### B.3 Access levels

Access to each section is one of five levels, in this order:

**none < view < operate < approve < manage**

The level a step requires is reached by that level or a higher one. A section level is necessary unless a step rule (§4.3) or an approval policy (§8.6) grants the step, and it is never sufficient on its own: the user's scope and the workflow's step rules also apply. Step rules add authority and never remove it.

How the business words in the grid map onto levels:

- "Override", "Approve / Override" and "Recommend + approve within limit" are **approve**. "Within limit" is the value band on the approval policy (§8.6), not a separate level.
- "Monitor" is **view**.
- "Configure" is **manage**.
- "Draft", "Mark damage only" and "Expenses only (create)" are **operate**: a write narrowed by record scope.

### B.5 Decided rules

1. **Receiving at a store.** A Store Person may receive goods at their own store, including uploading the vendor bill. PT-making stays warehouse work.
2. **Warehouse makes PTs.** Warehouse holds approve on Receive Goods because PT-making requires that level and Warehouse is the only operating role that reaches it. Lowering it would leave nobody able to make a PT.
3. **PT approval.** By default the Owner approves the PT, and the approver must be a different person from the preparer: one gate. A setting lets the administrator choose who may approve — office (the Owner role), warehouse (the Warehouse role) or either — and add an optional second check in which the warehouse checks the file before the office approves. Whatever the setting, the preparer never approves their own PT (§7.5 R-PT-005, §10.2, §15.2). The PT-approval step rule (§4.3) gives the Owner this authority, although the Owner's Receive Goods level is view.
4. **Bookings at a store.** Store Person has view on Booking so that a store can read the bookings headed to it and plan space and staff. It is read-only; placing a booking stays with head office and the Brand Manager.
5. **Booking cost is hidden** from Store Person and Warehouse (§10.4).
6. **Admin has no Money access.**
7. **Other approvals.** Approval of stock requests, count variances and transfers is open to the Owner and the Brand Manager.
8. **Small count variances.** The store's approval band for small count variances belongs to the Store Person.
9. **Product master and buying.** The Brand Manager carries the product master owner and buyer/merchandiser responsibilities (§4.2 C-PMO, C-BUY) for their own brands; the Owner carries them for a brand with no Brand Manager. The product master owner never reviews an item they proposed themselves.
10. **Quality control at receiving.** The Brand Manager owns wrong-article and damaged-in-transit exceptions for their own brands (§7.13 R-CTL-005); Warehouse owns them for a brand with no Brand Manager.
11. **Export, restore drills and monitoring.** The Owner or an Admin, each with step-up, may run a data export (§7.14 R-QLT-004), run a restore drill (R-QLT-003) or use the monitoring screen (R-QLT-007). Because Admin has no Money access (rule 6), an Admin-run export leaves out Money data; an Owner-run export is complete.
12. **Platform administrator.** The platform administrator (§4.2 X-PLT) is a separate support identity, not the Admin role; platform access alone cannot create tenant staff or review a tenant's privileged changes, apart from the tenant's first Owner, created once at onboarding (§16.6).

## Appendix C. Golden calculation cases

This appendix is normative. It holds the approved expected results for the receipt-costing rule in §7.3. The cases were worked out by hand from that rule and approved on 14 September 2026; they were never produced by running an implementation. They follow the golden-case discipline of §7.7 R-PRC-007: cases are versioned and immutable, a revised expected outcome requires explicit approval and keeps the earlier case, and expected answers are never regenerated from an implementation to make a check pass.

Rates, slabs and HSN codes below are synthetic test inputs, not a tax-rate declaration. Approving a case confirms that its expected numbers are right for the pinned profile. It does not approve the synthetic rates, slab boundaries or HSN codes as tenant configuration; those remain versioned tenant inputs (§7.3 R-MDM-006).

### C.1 Rules the cases apply

- `t` is the transport %, `g` the GST % of the selected slab and `m` the pricing margin %, as fractions.
- **Base-to-ticket:** `P RATE = round_paise(BASIC × (1 + t))`; `MRP = round_rupee(P RATE × (1 + g) ÷ (1 − m))`.
- **Ticket-to-purchase:** `P RATE = round_paise(MRP ÷ (1 + g) × (1 − m))`; `BASIC = P RATE`. No intermediate rounding.
- **Both-supplied:** `P RATE = round_paise(BASIC × (1 + t))`; the supplied MRP is kept.
- **Displayed MARGIN** = `round_2dp((MRP − P RATE) ÷ MRP × 100)`. It is a view, never an input.
- All rounding is half-up. P RATE is the layer cost.
- **Slabs (base-to-ticket):** work out a candidate MRP under every slab of the HSN, keep the slabs whose candidate falls inside that slab's own range, pick the lowest MRP if more than one qualifies, and block the row as a slab gap if none does. With a supplied MRP (ticket-to-purchase, both-supplied) the slab is the one that MRP falls in.
- A receipt or opening line needs P RATE > 0, MRP > 0 and P RATE ≤ MRP. Money stays within ₹0.00–₹999,999,999.99, quantity within 1–999,999, percentages within 0.00–99.99.
